**5th** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Manipulate
**Range** 120 feet | **Target** 1 creature | **Defense** Fortitude

[[Rank 5th]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]] [[Fortitude]]

You force a creature to experience an instantaneous burst of severely multiplied gravity, wreaking havoc on their eyes and internal organs. The target takes 2d12 bludgeoning damage and 1d12 [persistent bleed damage](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=86) with a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.
**Success** The target takes half damage, is [dazzled](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=65) for 1 round, and is [stunned](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=93) 1.
**Failure** The target takes full damage, is dazzled for 1 minute, and is stunned 2.
**Critical Failure** The target takes double damage, is dazzled for 1 minute, and is stunned 3.

### Heightened
**Heightened (+2)** The bludgeoning damage increases by 1d12, and the persistent bleed damage increases by 1d12.
