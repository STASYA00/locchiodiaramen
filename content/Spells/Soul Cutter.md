[[Rank 3rd]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]] [[AC]]

You conjure a blade of spiritual energy and cut at the soul of a foe. Make a melee spell attack against your target’s AC. On a success, you deal spirit damage. On a critical success, the target takes double damage. The number of actions you spend when Casting this Spell determines the range, damage, and other parameters. An adjacent target takes 3d6 spirit damage. An adjacent target takes 6d6 spirit damage, and on a hit, it becomes [enfeebled](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=71) 1 for 1 round. The blade flies out from you and attacks a target within 30 feet, dealing 6d6 spirit damage. The spell deals half damage on a failure (but not a critical failure), as the blade’s divine influence guides your attack. Unless you critically fail, the target becomes enfeebled 1 for 1 round.

### Heightened
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d6 for the 1-action version, or by 2d6 for the 2-and 3-action versions.
