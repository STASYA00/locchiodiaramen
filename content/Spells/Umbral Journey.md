Source: [Umbral Journey on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1729)

**5th** | Uncommon | Traits: Concentrate, Manipulate, Shadow, Teleportation, Uncommon
**Target** you and up to 10 willing creatures touched

[[Rank 5th]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]]

You move partially into the Netherworld, using its warped nature to speed your travels. Each hour, you cover roughly as much ground as you [normally would in 3 days](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2441). The landmarks are vague and symbolic rather than concrete, leaving you within a mile of your intended destination when you Dismiss the spell or its duration ends.
