[[Spells Index]]

Source: [Join Pasts on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=552)

**1st** | Uncommon | Traits: Cantrip, Divination, Mental, Uncommon
**Range** touch | **Target** 2 willing creatures

[[Rank 1st]] [[Occult]]

With touches to the targets’ foreheads, you bring them into mental communion. The targets can share thoughts and experiences, but not words. When one target attempts to [Recall Knowledge](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=26), the other can [Aid](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=75) the first target’s skill check, using any [Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=8) skill (even if that Lore wouldn’t normally apply) without having made any preparations to Aid.

### Heightened
**Heightened (+1)** You can touch one additional target to include them in the spell’s effects. Multiple targets can attempt checks to Aid the target who is attempting to Recall Knowledge.
