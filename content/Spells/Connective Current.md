[[Spells Index]]

Source: [Connective Current on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=2622)

**1st** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Manipulate
**Range** 30 feet | **Target** 1 enemy

[[Rank 1st]] [[Occult]]

You create a magical connection between you and an enemy, as if you were both caught in the same current. When the target takes a [move](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=658) action, you can [Stride](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2305) up to half your Speed in the same direction as the target as a reaction.

### Heightened
**Heightened (4th)** You can Stride up to your Speed.

**Heightened (6th)** As 4th, and you gain an additional reaction when you [Cast this Spell](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2734) and at the start of each of your turns that you can use only to make this movement.
