**3rd** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Manipulate

[[Rank 3rd]] [[Arcane]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]]

You become temporarily ghostlike as you push forward, moving through obstacles in your path. You gain a +10-foot status bonus to your Speeds, then Stride twice. While you don't become completely incorporeal, this movement ignores the effects of [difficult terrain](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=453), greater difficult terrain, and [hazardous terrain](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=454) and isn't impeded by physical barriers that don't block your line of sight. If the movement would end in a space that would normally be obstructed, it instead ends at the nearest unobstructed space you pass through before reaching it. You can use ghost rush to [Fly](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2312) or [Swim](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2381) instead of Stride if you have the corresponding movement type. If you're riding a mount or piloting a vehicle that's Large or smaller and unencumbered, you can cause the spell to affect the mount or vehicle instead, including any cargo or passengers.
