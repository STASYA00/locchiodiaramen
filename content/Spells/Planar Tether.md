Source: [Planar Tether on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1636)

**4th** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Manipulate
**Range** 30 feet | **Target** 1 creature | **Defense** Will

[[Rank 4th]] [[Arcane]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]] [[Will]]

You stitch the target to its current plane. While the target is affected by _planar tether_, the spell attempts to [counteract](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=371) any [teleportation](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=156) effect that would move the target, or any effect that would transport it to a different plane. _Planar tether's_ duration is determined by the target's Will save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.
**Success** The duration is 1 minute.
**Failure** The duration is 10 minutes.
**Critical Failure** The duration is 1 hour.
