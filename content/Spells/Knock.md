**2nd** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Manipulate
**Range** 30 feet | **Target** 1 door, lock, or container

[[Rank 2nd]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]]

You make the target easier to open. _Knock_ grants a +4 status bonus to any creature that tries to open the target door, lock, or container with an [Athletics](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=3) or a [Thievery](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=17) check. You can attempt a Thievery check to open the target as part of casting _knock_, and you add your level even if you're untrained.

 _Knock_ [counteracts](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=371) _lock_.
