Source: [Peaceful Bubble on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1624)

**4th** | Uncommon | Traits: Concentrate, Manipulate, Uncommon
**Range** touch | **Area** 100-foot burst

[[Rank 4th]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]]

An opaque shell of drifting, iridescent runes covers the area, creating a bubble. You can choose to make the burst smaller, in 10-foot increments. The runes don't block travel, but those inside the bubble can't perceive those outside it and vice versa. [Detection](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=43) and [scrying](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=141) effects are likewise blocked.

 Creatures sleeping within the bubble are immune to dreams sent by spells. Those sleeping 8 hours reduce their [doomed](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=9) condition by 2 instead of 1.
