**Range** 30 feet | **Target** 1 willing creature

[[Rank 1st]] [[Occult]]

You grace the target's mind, boosting its mental defenses and healing its wounds. The target regains 1d10+4 Hit Points when you Cast the Spell and gains a +2 status bonus to saves against [mental](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=106) effects for the duration.

### Heightened
**Heightened (+1)** The amount of healing increases by 1d10+4.
