Source: [Vision of Death on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1742)

**4th** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Death, Emotion, Fear, Manipulate, Mental
**Range** 120 feet | **Target** 1 living creature | **Defense** Will

[[Rank 4th]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]] [[Will]]

You force the target to see a vision of its own death. It takes 8d6 mental damage with a Will save. If the target is reduced to 0 HP by this spell, its vision becomes reality and kills it instantly.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.
**Success** The target takes half damage and is [frightened 1](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=19).
**Failure** The target takes full damage and is frightened 2.
**Critical Failure** The target takes double damage, is frightened 4, and is [fleeing](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=17) for as long as it's frightened.

### Heightened
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 2d6.
