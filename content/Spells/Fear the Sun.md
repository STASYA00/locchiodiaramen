Source: [Fear the Sun on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=2350)

**2nd** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Manipulate
**Range** 30 feet | **Target** 1 creature | **Defense** Fortitude

[[Rank 2nd]] [[Arcane]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]] [[Fortitude]]

You cause the creature’s vision to become particularly sensitive. The creature must attempt a Fortitude save. The creature is then temporarily immune for 1 minute.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected
**Success** The creature is [dazzled](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=65) for 1 round.
**Failure** The creature is dazzled for 1 minute.
**Critical Failure** The creature gains [light blindness](https://2e.aonprd.com/MonsterAbilities.aspx?ID=68) for 1 minute. If the creature is already exposed to [bright light](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2402), it immediately becomes [blinded](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=59) until the end of its next turn, as it isn’t acclimated to its newly acquired light blindness.

### Heightened
**Heightened (6th)** You can target up to 10 creatures.
