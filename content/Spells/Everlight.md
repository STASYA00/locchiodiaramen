**2nd** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Light, Manipulate
**Range** touch | **Target** a gemstone worth 6 gp or more

[[Rank 2nd]] [[Arcane]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]]

The gemstone you touch glows, spreading [bright light](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=407) with a color of your choice in a 20-foot radius (and [dim light](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=408) for the next 20 feet). The spell ends immediately if the gemstone is [broken](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=2).
