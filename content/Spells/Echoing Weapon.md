**1st** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Manipulate, Sonic
**Range** touch | **Target** 1 weapon

[[Rank 1st]] [[Arcane]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]]

You channel energy into the target weapon, and the air around it faintly hums each time it strikes a blow, as the impact is absorbed into the weapon. If a creature is wielding the weapon at the end of its turn, the weapon discharges a burst of sound targeting one creature adjacent to the wielder. The sonic damage dealt is equal to the number of successful [Strikes](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2306) with the target weapon that the wielder made that turn (to a maximum of 4 sonic damage if the wielder hits with four Strikes).

### Heightened
**Heightened (+2)** The sonic damage increases by 1 per Strike (and the maximum damage increases by 4).
