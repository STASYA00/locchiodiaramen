Source: [Day's Weight on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=2669)

**3rd** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Manipulate
**Range** 120 feet | **Target** 1 living creature | **Defense** Fortitude

[[Rank 3rd]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]] [[Fortitude]]

You fast-forward time for a single creature, burdening it with the aches and pain of an entire day and temporarily making it tired and weak. The creature must attempt a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.
**Success** The target is [fatigued](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=73) and [enfeebled 1](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=71) for 1 round.
**Failure** The target is fatigued and enfeebled 1 for the duration.
**Critical Failure** The target is fatigued and enfeebled 2 for the duration. The fatigued condition doesn't end when the spell's duration does (but can still be removed with a night's rest or by similar means).

### Heightened
**Heightened (6th)** You can target up to 10 creatures.
