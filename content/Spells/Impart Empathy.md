[[Spells Index]]

Source: [Impart Empathy on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1094)

**2nd** | Uncommon | Traits: Emotion, Enchantment, Mental, Uncommon
**Range** 30 feet | **Target** 1 animal | **Defense** Will

[[Rank 2nd]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]] [[Will]]

You expand the target's ability to understand social cues that are normally beyond its comprehension. Any creature to which the target isn't [unfriendly](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=40) or [hostile](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=23) can use [Diplomacy](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=6) to [Make an Impression](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=50) on it and to make very simple [Requests](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=51) of it. This doesn't render the animal any more inclined to help than it otherwise would be.

### Heightened
**Heightened (4th)** The spell can also target [plants](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=125) and [fungi](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=77).
