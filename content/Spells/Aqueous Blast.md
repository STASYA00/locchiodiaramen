**1st** | Rare | Traits: Evocation, Rare, Water

[[Rank 1st]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]]

You evoke a mass of water into the air around your outstretched fist. For the remainder of your turn, you can blast targets within 30 feet with this water by spending a single action which has the [attack](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=15) and [concentrate](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=32) traits. When you do so, attempt a ranged spell attack roll. If you hit, you inflict 2d8 bludgeoning damage. On a critical hit, the blast knocks the target [prone](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=31).

### Heightened
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d8.
