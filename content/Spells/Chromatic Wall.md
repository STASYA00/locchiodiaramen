**5th** | Common | Traits: Abjuration
**Range** 120 feet

[[Rank 5th]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]]

You create an opaque wall of light in a single vibrant color. The wall is straight and vertical, stretching 60 feet long and 30 feet high. If the wall would pass through a creature, the spell is lost. The wall sheds bright light for 20 feet on each side, and dim light for the next 20 feet. You can ignore the wall's effects.

 Roll 1d4 to determine the color of the wall. Each color has a particular effect on items, effects, or creatures that attempt to pass through. _Chromatic wall_ can't be [counteracted](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=371) normally; rather, each color is automatically counteracted when targeted by a specific spell, even if that spell's level is lower than that of _chromatic wall_. **Red** The wall destroys ranged weapon ammunition (such as arrows and crossbow bolts) that would pass through, and it deals 20 fire damage to anyone passing through, with a [basic](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=329) Reflex save. [_Cone of cold_](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=47) can counteract a red _chromatic wall_. **Orange** The wall destroys [thrown](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=195) weapons that would pass through, and it deals 25 acid damage to anyone passing through, with a basic Reflex save. [_Gust of wind_](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=143) can counteract an orange _chromatic wall_. **Yellow** The wall stops [acid](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=3), [cold](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=27), [electricity](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=56), [fire](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=72), [force](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=75), [negative](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=118), [positive](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=128), and [sonic](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=147) effects from passing through, and it deals 30 electricity damage to anyone passing through, with a basic Reflex save. [_Disintegrate_](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=76) can counteract a yellow _chromatic wall_. **Green** The wall stops toxins, gases, and breath weapons from passing through. It deals 10 poison damage to anyone passing through and makes them [enfeebled 1](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=13) for 1 minute. A basic Fortitude save reduces the damage and negates the enfeebled condition on a success. [_Passwall_](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=216) can counteract a green _chromatic wall_.

### Heightened
**Heightened (7th)** The spell's duration increases to 1 hour. Roll 1d8 to determine the wall's color; the results for 5–8 are below. A red, orange, yellow, or green wall deals an extra 10 damage. **Blue** The wall stops [auditory](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=16), petrification, [sonic](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=147), and [visual](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=163) effects from passing through, and creatures passing through are subject to the effects of [_flesh to stone_](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=123). [_Magic missile_](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=180) can counteract a blue _chromatic wall_. **Indigo** The wall stops [divination](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=47) and [mental](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=106) effects from passing through, and those passing through are subject to the effects of [_warp mind_](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=369). [_Searing light_](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=269) can counteract an indigo _chromatic wall_. **Violet** The wall prevents spells from targeting the other side (area effects still cross as normal). Creatures passing through must succeed at a Will save or they are [slowed 1](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=35) for 1 minute; on a critical failure, the creature is instead sent to another plane, with the effect of [_plane shift_](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=222) . [_Dispel magic_](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=78) can counteract a violet _chromatic wall_. Reroll, and creatures that pass through the wall take a –2 circumstance penalty to their saves.
