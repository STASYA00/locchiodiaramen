**1st** | Common | Traits: Air, Concentrate, Manipulate
**Range** 30 feet | **Area** 30-foot {{rules 2385 "burst"}}

[[Rank 1st]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]]

The air around you falls still and blocks out the sounds of the outside world. While you can clearly hear anything within this isolated dome, you can't hear anything outside of it. The opposite is also true, with no one outside of your dome being able to hear what's within. This dome doesn't block [line of sight](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2383). If anything larger than 1 Bulk passes through it, the dome is automatically dispersed. You can [Dismiss](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2311) this spell.

### Heightened
**Heightened (3rd)** The duration increases to 8 hours.

**Heightened (5th)** The duration increases to 24 hours.
