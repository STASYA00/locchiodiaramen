Source: [Thundering Dominance on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=2842)

**2nd** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Manipulate, Mental
**Range** 30 feet | **Target** your companion or eidolon

[[Rank 2nd]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]]

You shroud the target in a powerful predatory aura and amplify its vocalizations. It gains a +1 status bonus to [Intimidation](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=40) checks. Once during this spell’s duration, it can take the Thundering Roar action.**Thundering Roar**  ([auditory](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=541), [concentrate](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=561), [emotion](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=590), [fear](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=598), [mental](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=647), [sonic](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=697)) The target unleashes a powerful vocalization. Each enemy in a 10-foot emanation takes 4d8 sonic damage with a [basic](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2297) Will save against your spell DC. A creature that fails its Will save is also [frightened](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=76) 1.

### Heightened
**Heightened (+2)** The status bonus to Intimidation checks increases by 1 (to a maximum of a +4 status bonus for an 8th-rank spell), and the damage from Thundering Roar increases by 2d8.
