**5th** | Common | Traits: Death, Manipulate, Void, Negative
**Range** 30 feet | **Target** 1 living creature | **Defense** basic Fortitude

[[Rank 5th]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]] [[Fortitude]]

You supernaturally rip the spirit from a living creature’s body, dooming the target to pain and death. The target takes 5d6 void damage, depending on its basic Fortitude save, and is also [drained](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=68) 1 if it fails its save. The spell’s effect is based on how many actions you spend when Casting the Spell. The spell targets one living creature in range. (concentrate) The spell targets one living creature in range, and the damage is 10d6 void damage instead of 5d6. (concentrate) The spell targets all living creatures in a 30-foot emanation.

### Heightened
**Heightened (+1)** The amount of damage increases by 1d6, or by 2d6 for the 2-action version.
