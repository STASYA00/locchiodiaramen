**1st** | Common | Traits: Auditory, Concentrate, Linguistic, Manipulate, Mental
**Range** 30 feet | **Target** 1 creature | **Defense** Will

[[Rank 1st]] [[Arcane]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]] [[Will]]

You shout a command that's hard to ignore. You can command the target to approach you, run away (as if it had the [fleeing](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=17) condition), release what it's holding, drop [prone](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=31), or stand in place. It can't [Delay](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=77) or take any reactions until it has obeyed your command. The effects depend on the target's Will save.

**Success** The creature is unaffected.
**Failure** The creature must spend the first action on its next turn to obey your command.
**Critical Failure** The target must use all its actions on its next turn to obey your command.

### Heightened
**Heightened (5th)** You can target up to 10 creatures.
