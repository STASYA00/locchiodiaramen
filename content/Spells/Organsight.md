[[Spells Index]]

Source: [Organsight on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=963)

**3rd** | Common | Traits: Divination, Revelation
**Range** 30 feet | **Target** 1 living or undead creature that has organs

[[Rank 3rd]] [[Arcane]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]]

You see the target as though it's dissected and arrayed before you. For the duration, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus on [Medicine](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=9) checks against the target that depend on its organs, but a –2 circumstance penalty on Medicine checks depending on seeing its skin.

 When you Cast the Spell, attempt a special [Recall Knowledge](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=26) check using Medicine to spot and discern a vital organ. If you have a [Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=8) skill appropriate to the creature, you can use that skill instead of Medicine. If you succeed, the next time you deal piercing or slashing damage to the target with a Strike or spell, you deal 4d6 additional precision damage. Once on each of your subsequent turns, you can use a single action to attempt the special Recall Knowledge check again. The extra damage isn't cumulative, so making the check more than once before a Strike or spell has no extra benefit.

### Heightened
**Heightened (+1)** The precision damage increases by 1d6.
