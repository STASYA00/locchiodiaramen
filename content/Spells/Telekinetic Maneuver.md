**2nd** | Common | Traits: Attack, Concentrate, Force, Manipulate
**Range** 60 feet | **Target** 1 creature

[[Rank 2nd]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]]

With a rush of telekinetic power, you move a foe or something they carry. You can attempt to [Disarm](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=41), [Reposition](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2379), [Shove](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=38), or [Trip](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=40) the target using a spell attack roll instead of an [Athletics](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=3) check.
