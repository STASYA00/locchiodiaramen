Source: [Deathless March on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1910)

**4th** | Rare | Traits: Auditory, Enchantment, Rare
**Area** 20-foot emanation | **Defense** Will

[[Rank 4th]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]] [[Will]]

You chant a marching song of Yled to empower the undead and terrify the living. Undead creatures within the area gain the [quickened](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=32) condition for 1 minute, which they can use only to Stride. Living creatures in the area must attempt a Will save. This gains the [emotion](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=60), [_fear_](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=110), and [mental](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=106) traits for living creatures.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.
**Success** The creature is [frightened](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=19) 1.
**Failure** The creature is [frightened](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=19) 2.
**Critical Failure** The creature is [frightened](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=19) 3 and [paralyzed](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=28) for 1 round.
