**2nd** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Earth, Manipulate

[[Rank 2nd]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]]

You become aware of vibrations in the ground around you. You gain [tremorsense](https://2e.aonprd.com/MonsterAbilities.aspx?ID=40) as an [imprecise](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=412) sense with a range of 30 feet. As normal for tremorsense, you can detect a creature only if it's on the same surface as you and only if the subject is moving along (or burrowing through) the surface.

### Heightened
**Heightened (3rd)** The spell's duration is 1 hour.

**Heightened (5th)** The spell's duration is 8 hours.
