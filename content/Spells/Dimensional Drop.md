Source: [Dimensional Drop on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=2676)

**2nd** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Extradimensional, Manipulate, Teleportation
**Range** 60 feet | **Target** 1 creature standing on the ground | **Defense** Reflex

[[Rank 2nd]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]] [[Reflex]]

You place a portal on the ground directly beneath a creature. That target immediately plummets into extradimensional space, falling a distance through an infinite, featureless void before being forcibly ejected from a portal that appears right above their original space and taking the appropriate [fall damage](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=402). The target must attempt a Reflex saving throw. The DC to Arrest a Fall is equal to your spell DC.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.
**Success** The target falls 10 feet through the void before reappearing.
**Failure** The target falls 20 feet through the void before reappearing.
**Critical Failure** The target falls 40 feet through the void before reappearing.

### Heightened
**Heightened (5th)** You can target up to 5 creatures within range.
