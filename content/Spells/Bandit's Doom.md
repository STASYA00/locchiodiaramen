**5th** | Common | Traits: Abjuration, Curse
**Target** 1 item of 2 Bulk or less

[[Rank 5th]] [[Arcane]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]]

You ward the target item against those who would steal it. When a creature attempts to take the target into its possession, it takes 8d8 mental damage and is [doomed](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=9), depending on the result of its Will save. The spell then ends. You can choose up to 10 creatures when you cast this spell; if you do, those creatures can take the target item without triggering the ward. You can only have one _bandit's doom_ active at a time. If you cast the spell again, the newer casting supersedes the older.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.
**Success** The creature takes half damage and is doomed 1.
**Failure** The creature takes full damage and is doomed 2.
**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage and is doomed 3.

### Heightened
**Heightened (+2)** The damage increases by 2d8.
