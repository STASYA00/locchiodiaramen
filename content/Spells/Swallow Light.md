Source: [Swallow Light on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1209)

**2nd** | Uncommon | Traits: Cold, Darkness, Evocation, Negative, Uncommon
**Range** 30 feet | **Target** 1 magical _light_ effect

[[Rank 2nd]] [[Arcane]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]]

You draw magical light into your open mouth, consuming it and turning its essence into fuel for a gout of life-draining shadows. Attempt a [counteract](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=371) check against the light effect. If you successfully counteract it, you gain a breath weapon of chilling shadows you can use until the spell ends. Exhaling the breath is a two-action activity. The breath weapon is a 15-foot cone and deals 1d6 cold damage plus 1d6 negative damage. Creatures in the area can attempt a [basic](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=329) Reflex save against your spell DC. Once activated, you can't use the breath weapon again for 1d4 rounds.

### Heightened
**Heightened (+2)** Increase the damage of the breath weapon by 1d6 cold damage and 1d6 negative damage.
