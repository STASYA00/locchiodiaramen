You conjure a horrific cloud of tiny dancing bone shards that collapses like a torrent of rain upon those unfortunate enough to be nearby. Creatures in the area take 10d4 piercing damage and 5 [persistent bleed damage](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=29) ([basic](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=329) Reflex save).

### Heightened
**Heightened (+1)** The piercing damage increases by 2d4, and the persistent bleed damage increases by 1.
