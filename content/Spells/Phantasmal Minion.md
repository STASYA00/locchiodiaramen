**1st** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Manipulate, Summon
**Range** 60 feet

[[Rank 1st]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]]

You summon a [phantasmal minion](https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=2750). The minion is roughly the shape of a humanoid. You can choose to have it be [invisible](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=26) or have an ephemeral appearance, but it's obviously a magical effect, not a real creature.
