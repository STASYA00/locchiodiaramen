**4th** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Manipulate, Sonic
**Range** 100 feet | **Target** 1 living creature | **Defense** Fortitude

[[Rank 4th]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]] [[Fortitude]]

You send powerful sound waves through an opponent’s body, vibrating its bones and internal organs painfully. The creature takes 8d6 sonic damage and must attempt a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.
**Success** The target takes half damage.
**Failure** The target takes full damage, is [sickened 1](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=91), and is [deafened](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=66) for 1 round.
**Critical Failure** The target takes double damage, is sickened 2, and is deafened for 1 minute.

### Heightened
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 2d6.
