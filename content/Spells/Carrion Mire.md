Source: [Carrion Mire on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1909)

**2nd** | Uncommon | Traits: Conjuration, Negative, Uncommon
**Range** 30 feet | **Area** 15-foot burst | **Defense** Reflex

[[Rank 2nd]] [[Arcane]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]] [[Reflex]]

The ground opens into a seemingly endless mass of corpse limbs, grasping at all creatures within the area to drag them down. Creatures in the area when the spell is cast or beginning their turn in the area must succeed at a Reflex save or fall [prone](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=88). When a creature in the spell's areas would be healed by a [vitality](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=509) effect, the _carrion mire_ attempts to [counteract](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=371) that effect, preventing that healing but not otherwise prevent the effect if it's counteracted.
