**2nd** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Manipulate, Mental
**Range** 30 feet | **Target** 1 creature | **Defense** Will

[[Rank 2nd]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]] [[Will]]

You dull the target's mind, depending on its Will save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.
**Success** The target is [stupefied 1](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=37) until the start of your next turn.
**Failure** The target is stupefied 2 for 1 minute.
**Critical Failure** The target is stupefied 3 for 1 minute.
