**1st** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Manipulate, Prediction
**Range** 30 feet | **Target** 1 creature

[[Rank 1st]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]]

You grant the target brief foresight. The target gains a +1 status bonus to its next [initiative](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=3300) roll, after which the spell ends.

### Heightened
**Heightened (+2)** The status bonus increases by 1, to a maximum of +4 at 7th rank.
