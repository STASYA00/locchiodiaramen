[[Spells Index]]

Source: [Chosen Gravity on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=2654)

**3rd** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Manipulate
**Range** 30 feet | **Target** 1 creature

[[Rank 3rd]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]]

The target can change the direction gravity pulls them after they touch a surface with their feet. The target can walk on vertical surfaces or upside down as though gravity was pulling them toward the surface. In addition, the target gains a +1 status bonus to their saving throws and Fortitude DC against [forced movement](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=451). When the spell ends, the target is affected by gravity as normal and might fall if not on the floor.

### Heightened
**Heightened (6th)** The duration increases to 1 hour.
