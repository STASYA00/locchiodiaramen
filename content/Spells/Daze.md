**1st** | Common | Traits: Cantrip, Concentrate, Manipulate, Mental, Nonlethal
**Range** 60 feet | **Target** 1 creature | **Defense** Will

[[Rank 1st]] [[Arcane]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]] [[Will]]

You push into the target's mind and daze it with a mental jolt. The jolt deals 1d6 mental damage, with a basic Will save. If the target critically fails the save, it is also [stunned 1](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=36).

### Heightened
**Heightened (+2)** The damage increases by 1d6.
