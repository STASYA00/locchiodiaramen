**5th** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Manipulate
**Range** 60 feet | **Target** 1 creature | **Defense** Fortitude

[[Rank 5th]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]] [[Fortitude]]

You point at a target in range, and its skin grows searing blisters filled with caustic fluid. The target must attempt a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.
**Success** The target grows one blister. You can spend a single action, which has the [concentrate](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=561) trait, to pop a blister. The target and each creature in a 15-foot cone originating from the target takes 7d6 acid damage ([basic](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=329) Fortitude save). You choose the direction of the cone, which can't include the target. When no blisters are left, the spell ends.
**Failure** As success, but the target grows two blisters.
**Critical Failure** As success, but the target grows four blisters.

### Heightened
**Heightened (+1)** The damage of a popped blister increases by 1d6.
