[[Spells Index]]

Source: [Laughing Fit on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1583)

**2nd** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Emotion, Manipulate, Mental
**Range** 30 feet | **Target** 1 living creature | **Defense** Will

[[Rank 2nd]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]] [[Will]]

The target is overtaken with uncontrollable laughter. It must attempt a Will save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.
**Success** The target is plagued with uncontrollable laughter. It can't use reactions.
**Failure** The target is [slowed 1](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=35) and can't use reactions.
**Critical Failure** The target falls [prone](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=31) and can't use actions or reactions for 1 round. It then takes the effects of a failure.
