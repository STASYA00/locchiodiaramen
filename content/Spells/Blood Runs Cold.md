**2nd** | Common | Traits: Cold, Concentrate, Manipulate
**Range** 60 feet | **Target** 1 creature | **Defense** Fortitude

[[Rank 2nd]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]] [[Fortitude]]

You chill the blood in the target's veins. The target takes 1d6 [persistent cold damage](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=29) with a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.
**Success** The target takes half the persistent cold damage.
**Failure** The target takes the full persistent cold damage and is [slowed 1](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=92) for 1 round.
**Critical Failure** The target takes double the persistent cold damage and is slowed 1 until the persistent cold damage ends.

### Heightened
**Heightened (+2)** Increase the persistent cold damage by 1d6.
