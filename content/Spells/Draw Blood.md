**2nd** | Common | Traits: Attack, Concentrate, Manipulate, Vitality
**Range** touch | **Target** 1 living creature | **Defense** AC

[[Rank 2nd]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]] [[AC]]

The palm of your hand grows a mouth with impossibly long fangs that ache to drink your enemies' blood. Make a melee spell attack against a target. On a hit, you deal 4d4 piercing damage and 2 [persistent bleed damage](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=29) (or double damage on a critical hit). For the duration of the spell, you regain 2 Hit Points each time the target takes bleed damage.

### Heightened
**Heightened (+1)** The piercing damage increases by 2d4, and the persistent bleed damage and healing increase by 1.
