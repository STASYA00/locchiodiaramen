Source: [Inkshot on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1229)

**1st** | Rare | Traits: Conjuration, Poison, Rare
**Range** 30 feet

[[Rank 1st]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]]

A spray of viscous, toxic ink jets from your fingertip to strike a target creature in the face. Make a spell attack roll against the target. On a hit, you deal 2d6 poison damage, plus you blast the target's eyes, making them [dazzled](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=7) for 1 round as the stinging ink blurs and distorts the creature's vision. On a critical hit, double the poison damage, and the target becomes dazzled for 1 minute by the foul ink. 

The ink stain remains for 1 hour before fading, although vigorous cleansing (or magic such as a [_prestidigitation_](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=229) cantrip) can remove the ink before then.

### Heightened
**Heightened (+1)** Increase the base poison damage by 2d6.
