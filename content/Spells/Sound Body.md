**2nd** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Healing, Manipulate, Vitality
**Range** touch | **Target** 1 willing creature

[[Rank 2nd]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]]

You send a surge of healing energy to restore the target's body. Attempt to [counteract](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=371) an effect of your choice imposing one of these conditions on the target: [blinded](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=1), [dazzled](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=7), [deafened](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=8), [enfeebled](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=13), or [sickened](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=34). If you didn't counteract the effect, but you would have if its counteract rank were 2 lower, instead suppress the effect until the beginning of your next turn. The effect's duration doesn't elapse while it's suppressed. This spell can't counteract or suppress [curses](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=38), [diseases](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=46), or conditions that are part of the target's normal state.

### Heightened
**Heightened (4th)** Add [drained](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=68) and [slowed](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=92) to the list of conditions.

**Heightened (6th)** As 4th rank, plus add [petrified](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=87).

**Heightened (8th)** As 4th rank, plus add [petrified](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=87) and [stunned](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=93).
