**2nd** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Manipulate
**Range** 30 feet | **Target** 1 creature | **Defense** Fortitude

[[Rank 2nd]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]] [[Fortitude]]

Even the fundamental forces of the universe can bend to your magic and submit to your will. You create an isolated but powerful downward force by greatly increasing the force of gravity your target experiences, hampering its movement and pushing it to the ground. The target must attempt a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.
**Success** The target is [encumbered](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=70) for 1 round.
**Failure** The target falls [prone](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=88) and is encumbered for 1 round. Additionally, if this spell causes the target to fall, the DC to [Arrest a Fall](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2308) is your spell DC.
**Critical Failure** The target falls prone and is encumbered for 1 round. Additionally, if this spell causes the target to fall, the target can’t Arrest a Fall, and falling damage is doubled.

### Heightened
**Heightened (4th)** You can target up to five creatures.
