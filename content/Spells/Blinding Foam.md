Source: [Blinding Foam on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1279)

**5th** | Uncommon | Traits: Acid, Evocation, Incapacitation, Uncommon
**Range** 60 feet | **Target** 1 creature

[[Rank 5th]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]]

You spray a colorful, caustic foam into the target's eyes, which clings to their face. Make a spell attack roll. If you hit, you deal 5d10 acid damage and 1d6 [persistent acid damage](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=29), and the target is [blinded](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=1) for the duration. The foam can be removed with 3 Interact actions, which ends the persistent acid damage. These Interact actions don't need to be consecutive. When the target's persistent acid damage ends, the spell automatically ends.

### Heightened
**Heightened (8th)** The initial damage increases to 9d10, and the persistent acid damage increases to 2d6.
