**1st** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Emotion, Manipulate, Mental
**Range** 30 feet | **Target** 1 creature | **Defense** Will

[[Rank 1st]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]] [[Will]]

You sow seeds of confusion in your target’s mind, causing its actions and thoughts to become [clumsy](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=61).

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.
**Success** The target is clumsy 1 and [stupefied](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=94) 1.
**Failure** The target is clumsy 2 and stupefied 2.
**Critical Failure** The target is clumsy 3, stupefied 3, and [confused](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=63).
