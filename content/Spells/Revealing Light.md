**2nd** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Light, Manipulate
**Range** 120 feet | **Area** 10-foot burst | **Defense** Reflex

[[Rank 2nd]] [[Arcane]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]] [[Reflex]]

A wave of magical light washes over the area. You choose the appearance of the light, such as colorful, heatless flames or sparkling motes. A creature affected by revealing light is [dazzled](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=7). If the creature was [invisible](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=26), it becomes [concealed](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=4) instead. If the creature was already concealed for any other reason, it is no longer concealed.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.
**Success** The light affects the creature for 2 rounds.
**Failure** The light affects the creature for 1 minute.
**Critical Failure** The light affects the creature for 10 minutes.
