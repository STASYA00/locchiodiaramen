**2nd** | Uncommon | Traits: Auditory, Concentrate, Manipulate, Uncommon
**Area** 60-foot line | **Defense** Fortitude

[[Rank 2nd]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]] [[Fortitude]]

You tell the story of the Queen of Bees and her retinue trying to pass through a rainbow on their way to visit the King of Spiders. You conjure forth a large, transparent rainbow. Creatures who enter or begin their turn in the rainbow’s space must succeed at a Fortitude saving throw or become [dazzled](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=65) for 1 round (or [blinded](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=59) for 1 round on a critical failure).
