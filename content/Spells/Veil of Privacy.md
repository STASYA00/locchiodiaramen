**3rd** | Uncommon | Traits: Concentrate, Manipulate, Uncommon
**Range** touch | **Target** 1 creature or object

[[Rank 3rd]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]]

You erect protective wards that make the target difficult to detect via magic. _Veil of privacy_ attempts to [counteract](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=371) all [detection](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=43), [revelation](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=138), and [scrying](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=141) effects used against the target or the target's gear throughout the duration, counting cantrips as 1st-rank spells for this purpose. Successfully counteracting a spell that targets an area or multiple targets negates the effects for only _veil of privacy's_ target.
