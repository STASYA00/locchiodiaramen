Your eyes darken until they become entirely pitch black. You grant yourself the ability to see in the darkness. You gain [darkvision](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=415). If you already have darkvision, you gain greater darkvision.

### Heightened
**Heightened (7th)** The duration is until the next time you make daily preparations.
