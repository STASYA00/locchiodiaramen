**Area** 15-foot cone | **Defense** basic Fortitude

[[Rank 1st]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]] [[Fortitude]]

You echo a jarring hymn that only creatures in the area can hear. The hymn deals 1d8 sonic damage, with a basic Fortitude save. If a target critically fails the save, it's also [deafened](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=66) for 1 minute.

### Heightened
**Heightened (+2)** The damage increases by 1d8.
