Source: [Telepathy on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1719)

**4th** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Linguistic, Manipulate, Mental

[[Rank 4th]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]]

You can communicate telepathically with creatures within 30 feet. Once you establish a connection by communicating with a creature, the communication is two-way. You can communicate with only creatures that share a language with you.

### Heightened
**Heightened (6th)** You can communicate telepathically with creatures using shared mental imagery even if you don't share a language; _telepathy_ loses the [linguistic](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=101) trait.
