**5th** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Incapacitation, Manipulate, Mental
**Range** 120 feet | **Target** 1 creature | **Defense** Will

[[Rank 5th]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]] [[Will]]

You scramble the ability of the target to use language, causing them to speak in unintelligible tongues.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.
**Success** For 1 round, the target's language becomes unintelligible, they're unable to communicate ideas with other creatures, and any action they take with the [linguistic](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=642) trait fails.
**Failure** As success, and the target can't cast spells that require incantations for the duration. Spells with the [subtle](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=522) trait don't require incantations.
**Critical Failure** As failure, except the duration is 1 minute.
