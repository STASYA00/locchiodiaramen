**2nd** | Common | Traits: Attack, Concentrate, Manipulate
**Range** 60 feet | **Target** 1 creature | **Defense** AC

[[Rank 2nd]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]] [[AC]]

You send out a flock of crows that torments the target with beak and talon. Make a ranged spell attack against a target’s AC. On a hit, the target takes 6d4 piercing damage and is [dazzled](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=65) for 1 round (double damage and [blinded](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=59) for 1 round on a critical hit).

### Heightened
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 3d4.
