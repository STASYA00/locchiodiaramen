A Linnorm King once defended her kingdom from Irriseni invaders with a casting of this spell. You call upon the secret moon, who's always whole, to shine upon yourself, granting you the form of a mighty [werewolf](https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=3236) without letting the curse touch you. You transform yourself into a Large hybrid wolf battle form. While in this form, you gain the [beast](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=547) trait. This battle form doesn't prevent you from speaking, casting spells, or using your hands. You gain the following abilities.

### Heightened
**Heightened (+2)** The temporary Hit Points granted by this spell when you cast or Fuel it increase by 20. All unarmed Strikes granted by this spell deal an additional die of damage. Your attack modifier increases by +6. Finally, your AC increases by 2.
