[[Spells Index]]

Source: [Invoke True Name on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1065)

**1st** | Rare | Traits: Cantrip, Enchantment, Rare, True Name
**Range** 30 feet | **Target** 1 creature whose true name you know

[[Rank 1st]] [[Arcane]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]]

You speak the true name of a creature to more surely affect it with your magic. Until the end of the current turn, the target is [flat-footed](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=16) against your spells and takes a –2 circumstance penalty to saving throws against your spells. In addition, the first time this turn the target takes damage from one of your spells, it has weakness to that damage equal to double the level of _invoke true name_.
