Source: [Disguise Magic on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1491)

**1st** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Illusion, Manipulate
**Range** 30 feet | **Target** 1 item or spell effect

[[Rank 1st]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]]

You alter how an item's or spell's magical aura appears to effects like [_detect magic_](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=66). You can hide the auras entirely, have an item register as a common item of lower level, or make a spell register as a common spell of the same or lower rank. You can Dismiss the spell.

 A caster using _detect magic_ or [_read aura_](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=246) of a higher rank than _disguise magic_ can attempt to disbelieve the illusion using the skill matching the tradition of the spell ([Arcana](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=2) for [arcane](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=11), [Religion](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=13) for [divine](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=48), [Occultism](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=11) for [occult](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=120), or [Nature](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=10) for [primal](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=134)). Further attempts by the same caster get the same result as the initial check to disbelieve.

### Heightened
**Heightened (2nd)** You can Cast this Spell on a creature, disguising all items and spell effects on it.
