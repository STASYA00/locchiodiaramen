**3rd** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Manipulate, Teleportation
**Range** 100 miles | **Target** your familiar

[[Rank 3rd]] [[Arcane]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]]

Your familiar dissolves and rematerializes in your space. The exact appearance of this teleportation differs based on your tradition (a [druid's](https://2e.aonprd.com/Classes.aspx?ID=34) familiar might fall into a spray of autumn leaves, a shadowy familiar might simply fade away), but it's always clearly supernatural.

### Heightened
**Heightened (5th)** You can call your familiar so long as your familiar is on the same planet as you.

**Heightened (7th)** You can call your familiar so long as your familiar is on the same plane of existence as you.

**Heightened (9th)** You can call your familiar even if the familiar is on a different plane of existence.
