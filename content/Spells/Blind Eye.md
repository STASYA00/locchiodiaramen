[[Spells Index]]

Source: [Blind Eye on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1213)

**5th** | Uncommon | Traits: Illusion, Uncommon
**Target** 1 object

[[Rank 5th]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]]

You enchant a single object, preventing it from being used for magical observation. If you cast this spell on a non-magical item used to cast scrying spells, such as a [spell component pouch](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=33) or a spell focus, the item can't be used to cast the spell. If you cast _blind eye_ on a magical item that can be activated to scry (such as a [_crystal ball_](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=253) or [_hag eye_](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=935)), the item can't be activated for scrying effects. Magical items that are twice _blind eye's_ spell level or more aren't blocked this way.
