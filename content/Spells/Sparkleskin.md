**3rd** | Uncommon | Traits: Transmutation, Uncommon
**Range** touch | **Target** 1 willing creature

[[Rank 3rd]] [[Arcane]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]]

You coat a creature's body in a layer of brilliantly sparkling glitter. The target gains a +2 status bonus on [Deception](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=5) checks to [Make a Diversion](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=45) and to [Feint](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=48), and on [Performance](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=12) checks to [Perform](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=59) that have the [visual](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=163) trait. When the target take damage, glitter bursts out of its wound in a 5-foot emanation. Creatures in the area must attempt a Fortitude save.

**Failure** The creature is [dazzled](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=7) for 1 round.
**Critical Failure** The creature is dazzled for 3 rounds.
