**2nd** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Light, Manipulate, Sonic
**Range** 60 feet | **Area** varies | **Defense** basic Fortitude

[[Rank 2nd]] [[Arcane]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]] [[Fortitude]]

You create a small explosion of bright light and ear-piercing sound that rocks the senses. Creatures in the area take sonic damage with a [basic](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=329) Fortitude save. The number of actions you spend when Casting this Spell determines the damage and other parameters.

 This spell deals 1d10 sonic damage, and its area is a 5-foot square. Creatures that fail their save become [dazzled](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=65) until the end of your next turn. This spell deals 2d10 sonic damage, and its area is a 10- foot [burst](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2385). Creatures that fail their save become dazzled until the end of your next turn. This spell deals 2d10 sonic damage, and its area is a 20- foot burst. Creatures that fail their save become dazzled and [deafened](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=66) until the end of your next turn.

### Heightened
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d10 for all versions.
