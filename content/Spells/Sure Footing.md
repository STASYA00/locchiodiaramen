**2nd** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Healing, Manipulate
**Range** touch | **Target** 1 willing creature

[[Rank 2nd]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]]

You free the target's limbs from ailments that impede mobility. Attempt to [counteract](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=371) an effect of your choice imposing one of these conditions on the target: [clumsy](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=3), [grabbed](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=20), or [paralyzed](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=28). If you didn't counteract the effect, but you would have if its counteract rank were 2 lower, instead suppress the effect until the beginning of your next turn. The effect's duration doesn't elapse while it's suppressed.

 This spell can't counteract or suppress [curses](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=38), [diseases](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=46), or conditions that are part of the target's normal state.

### Heightened
**Heightened (4th)** Add [immobilized](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=81), [restrained](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=90), and [slowed](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=92) to the list of conditions.

**Heightened (6th)** As 4th rank, plus add [petrified](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=87).

**Heightened (8th)** As 4th rank, plus add [petrified](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=87) and [stunned](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=93).
