**1st** | Common | Traits: Cantrip, Concentrate, Manipulate

[[Rank 1st]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]]

When you walk, the earth warps beneath your feet—your steps extend, distance contracts, and everything is just a little bit closer. You gain a +5-foot status bonus to your Speed until the end of your turn. You then Stride twice. You can use warp step to [Burrow](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2310), [Climb](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2374), [Fly](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2312), or [Swim](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2381) instead of Stride if you have the corresponding movement type.
