**4th** | Uncommon | Traits: Concentrate, Detection, Manipulate, Uncommon
**Area** 30-foot emanation

[[Rank 4th]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]]

By reading trace auras, you detect the presence of [scrying](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=141) effects in the area. If _detect scrying_ is higher rank than a scrying effect, you gain a glimpse of the scrying creature and learn its approximate distance and direction.

### Heightened
**Heightened (6th)** The duration is until your next daily preparations.
