**3rd** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Disease, Manipulate
**Range** 60 feet | **Target** 1 creature with bones or a natural exoskeleton | **Defense** Fortitude

[[Rank 3rd]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]] [[Fortitude]]

A tiny, bone-white moth emerges from your open mouth. It shakes itself off, spreads its wings, and flies toward your foe, brushing its wings against their exposed skin. The moth carries a disease that causes a victim's bones to quickly grow soft and eventually melt. The target must attempt a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.
**Success** The moth only briefly brushes its wings against the target. The target is {{conditions 91 "sickened 1"}}.
**Failure** The moth lands on the target, kissing the spot with its fluttering. The target is afflicted with the bone moth's kiss at stage 1.
**Critical Failure** The moth lands on an open wound, targeting it with its kiss. The target is afflicted with the bone moth's kiss at stage 2.

 **Bone Moth's Kiss** ([disease](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=578)) **Stage 1** [drained 1](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=68) and [sickened 1](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=91) (1 round); **Stage 2** drained 2 (1 round); **Stage 3** drained 3 and can't recover from the drained condition until the disease is cured (1 day)
