[[Spells Index]]

Source: [Imprint Message on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=2003)

**1st** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Manipulate
**Range** touch | **Target** 1 object

[[Rank 1st]] [[Occult]]

You project psychic vibrations onto the target object, imprinting it with a short message or emotional theme of your design. This imprinted sensation is revealed to a creature who casts [_object reading_](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=705) on the target object, replacing any emotional events the item was present for. If the object is in the area of a [_retrocognition_](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1652) spell, the imprinted messages appear as major events in the timeline, but they don't interfere with any other visions.

 If the object is targeted with [_read aura_](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1646) of a higher spell rank than _imprint message_, the caster learns that the object has been magically modified. When you Cast this Spell, any prior vibrations placed on an object by previous castings of _imprint message_ fade.
