**3rd** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Manipulate, Polymorph
**Range** touch | **Target** your familiar

[[Rank 3rd]] [[Arcane]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]]

You hold your familiar in your arms and then throw them into the sky—whereupon they sprout wings and take flight. The wings look appropriate to the familiar’s nature. A tabby cat might simply grow a pair of grayish wings like a pigeon, while a frog grows membranous wings a bit like a bat. Your familiar gains a 20-foot fly Speed.Familiars who already have a fly Speed instead gain a +15-foot status bonus to their fly Speed.
