**4th** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Manipulate
**Range** 60 feet | **Area** 10-foot {{rules 2385 "burst"}}

[[Rank 4th]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]]

You create an echo of a powerful poltergeist, temporarily anchoring it to the area. Because the poltergeist is a manifestation and not truly present, it can’t be attacked or hurt; it remains [invisible](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=83) unless otherwise stated. When you Cast the Spell, and the first time you Sustain it on subsequent rounds, the poltergeist creates your choice of one of the following effects.

- **Deathly Assault** ([void](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=510)) The poltergeist focuses on one creature in the area and flies through that creature, dealing 4d10 void damage with a [basic](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2297) Fortitude save.
- **Frighten** ([emotion](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=590), [fear](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=598), [mental](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=647)) The poltergeist becomes visible, appearing as a skeletal, ghostlike humanoid. Creatures in the area must attempt a Will save, becoming [frightened 2](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=76) on a failure.
- **Telekinetic Storm** ([force](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=610)) The poltergeist hurls debris and quasi-real objects around in the area. All creatures in the area take 4d8 force damage with a [basic](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2297) Reflex save.

### Heightened
**Heightened (+2)** The damage of the deathly assault effect increases by 1d10, and the damage of the telekinetic storm effect increases by 1d8.
