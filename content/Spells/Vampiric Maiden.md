**4th** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Manipulate, Void
**Range** 30 feet | **Target** 1 creature | **Defense** Fortitude

[[Rank 4th]] [[Arcane]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]] [[Fortitude]]

A ghostly iron maiden snaps shut on the target and drains its vitality for your gain. This deals 4d4 piercing damage and 4d4 void damage, and the target must attempt a Fortitude save. You gain temporary Hit Points equal to the void damage the target takes (after applying resistances, weaknesses, and the like). You lose any remaining temporary Hit Points after 1 minute.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.
**Success** The target takes half damage.
**Failure** The target is briefly trapped within the vampiric maiden. The target takes full damage and is [immobilized](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=81) by the iron maiden for 1 round or until it uses an Interact action to extricate itself, whichever comes first.
**Critical Failure** The target takes double damage and is immobilized by the vampiric maiden for 1 round or until it [Escapes](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2296) (the DC is your spell DC), whichever comes first.

### Heightened
**Heightened (+1)** The piercing and void damage increase by 1d4 each.
