You invigorate the touched creature’s mind and urge it to press on. You grant the touched creature 5 [temporary Hit Points](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2321) that last for 1 minute.

### Heightened
**Heightened (+1)** The temporary Hit Points increase by 5.
