Source: [Needle Darts on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1375)

**1st** | Common | Traits: Attack, Cantrip, Concentrate, Manipulate, Metal
**Range** 60 feet | **Target** 1 creature | **Defense** AC

[[Rank 1st]] [[Arcane]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]] [[AC]]

You shape three needles out of a piece of metal in your possession and send them flying in a tight group toward one target. Make a spell attack roll against your target's AC. The needles deal 3d4 piercing damage and might cause bleeding. The needles impart any special properties of the metal that forms them; for instance, [cold iron](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=272) needles deal additional damage to creatures with weakness to cold iron. All the needles are made of the same metal, and the metal returns to you after the attack.

**Critical Success** The target takes double damage and 1 [persistent bleed damage](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=29).
**Success** The target takes full damage.

### Heightened
**Heightened (+1)** You send one additional needle, increasing the regular damage by 1d4 and increasing the persistent bleed damage on a critical hit by 1.
