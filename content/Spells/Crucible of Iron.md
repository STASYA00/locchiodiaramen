Source: [Crucible of Iron on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=2665)

**5th** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Fire, Manipulate, Metal
**Range** 60 feet | **Target** 1 creature | **Defense** Reflex

[[Rank 5th]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]] [[Reflex]]

You call forth a crucible of molten iron that pours over the target, burning them as the metal quickly covers and encases their body. The target must attempt a Reflex save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.
**Success** The target's head is encased in iron, causing them to be [dazzled](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=65) for 1 round and take 1d10 [persistent fire damage](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=29).
**Failure** The target's head, arms, and chest are all encased. The target is dazzled and [enfeebled 1](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=71) for 1 minute or until they [Escape](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2296). They also take 1d10 persistent fire damage.
**Critical Failure** The target's legs and feet are also encased. The target is dazzled, enfeebled 2, and [immobilized](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=81) for 1 minute or until they Escape. They also take 1d10 persistent fire damage.

### Heightened
**Heightened (+2)** The persistent fire damage increases by 1d10.
