Source: [Penumbral Shroud on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=2360)

**1st** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Darkness, Manipulate, Shadow
**Range** 30 feet | **Target** 1 creature | **Defense** Reflex

[[Rank 1st]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]] [[Reflex]]

You envelop the target in a shroud of shadow. The target perceives [light](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2401) as one step lower than it actually is ([bright light](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2402) becomes [dim light](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2403), for example), affecting their ability to sense creatures and objects accordingly. The shroud also provides the target a +1 status bonus to saving throws against [light](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=640) effects. This effect is helpful to creatures sensitive to light, and a creature can willingly choose to be subject to the failure effect of the spell.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.
**Success** The effect lasts for 1 round.
**Failure** The effect lasts its normal duration.
