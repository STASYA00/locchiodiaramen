**Range** 30 feet | **Target** 1 living creature | **Defense** basic Fortitude

[[Rank 1st]] [[Arcane]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]] [[Fortitude]]

You call upon the Void to harm life force. The target takes 2d4 void damage with a basic Fortitude save. On a critical failure, the target is also [enfeebled 1](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=13) until the start of your next turn.

### Heightened
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d4.
