**4th** | Uncommon | Traits: Manipulate, Uncommon
**Range** 40 feet | **Target** 1 creature in line of sight | **Defense** basic Fortitude

[[Rank 4th]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]] [[Fortitude]]

You cut your own body in a ritualistic manner, causing similar damage to a creature in your line of sight. These cuts are superficial and cause 1d4 slashing damage to you; however, the wounds that open up on our target’s body are far deeper. The targeted creature takes 5d8 slashing damage; a creature that critically fails this saving throw also takes 1d8 [persistent bleed damage](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=86). If you cast this as a three-action spell, the spell instead affects a 5-foot burst.

### Heightened
**Heightened (+1)** Increase the damage dealt to the target by 1d8, and increase the persistent bleed damage by 1.
