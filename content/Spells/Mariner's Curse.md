**5th** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Curse, Manipulate
**Target** 1 creature | **Defense** Will

[[Rank 5th]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]] [[Will]]

You afflict the target with the curse of the roiling, unforgiving sea. The target must attempt a Will save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.
**Success** The target becomes [sickened 1](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=34). Reducing its sickened condition to 0 ends the curse.
**Failure** The target becomes sickened 1 and can't reduce its sickened condition below 1 while the curse remains. The curse can be lifted by 4th-rank [_cleanse affliction_](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1467) or similar magic. Whenever the target is sickened and on the water at least a mile from shore, it is also [slowed 1](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=35).
**Critical Failure** As failure, but the target becomes sickened 2.
