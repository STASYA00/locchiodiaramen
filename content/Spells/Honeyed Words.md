**4th** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Manipulate, Mental

[[Rank 4th]] [[Occult]]

Falsehoods pass your lips as smoothly as silk. You gain a +4 status bonus to [Deception](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=5) checks to [Lie](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=47) and against Perception checks to discern if you are telling the truth, and you add your level even if untrained. If the implausibility of your lies prompts a circumstance penalty or a DC increase, reduce that penalty by half or increase it by half.
