**1st** | Uncommon | Traits: Cantrip, Conjuration, Uncommon

[[Rank 1st]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]]

You pull a large handful of guts from your midsection. Removing these guts doesn't harm you. You can use the guts as 50 feet of rope to help you [Climb](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=33), and they help attach you to a wall. You move half as quickly as usual while climbing with your guts (minimum 5 feet), but you can attempt a DC 5 flat check whenever you critically fail to prevent a fall. Other creatures can use your guts to climb like a squishy rope, but the guts don't help anyone but you attach to a wall. If you let go of your guts or your guts are damaged, the spell ends.

### Heightened
**Heightened (3rd)** You gain a +1 item bonus to [Athletics](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=3) checks to Climb while using your guts.
