**2nd** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Manipulate, Revelation

[[Rank 2nd]] [[Arcane]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]]

Your gaze pierces through illusions and finds [invisible](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=26) creatures and spirits. You can see invisible creatures as though they weren't invisible, although their features are blurred, making them [concealed](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=62) and difficult to identify. You can also see [incorporeal](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=222) creatures, like [ghosts](https://2e.aonprd.com/MonsterFamilies.aspx?ID=51), phased through an object from within 10 feet of an object's surface as blurry shapes seen through those objects. Subtler clues also grant you a +2 status bonus to checks you make to disbelieve [illusions](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=92).

### Heightened
**Heightened (5th)** This spell has a duration of 8 hours.
