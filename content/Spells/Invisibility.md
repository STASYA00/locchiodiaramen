**2nd** | Common | Traits: Illusion, Manipulate, Subtle
**Range** touch | **Target** 1 creature

[[Rank 2nd]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]]

Illusions bend light around the target, rendering it [invisible](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=26). This makes it [undetected](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=39) to all creatures, though the creatures can attempt to find the target, making it [hidden](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=22) to them instead. If the target uses a [hostile action](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=300), the spell ends after that hostile action is completed._ Paizo ClarificationInvisibility follows the rules of the invisible condition. The second sentence summarizes them but doesn't cover every instance, such as you being hidden to creatures observing you when you become invisible instead of being undetected to them.

### Heightened
**Heightened (4th)** The spell lasts 1 minute, but it doesn't end if the target uses a hostile action.
