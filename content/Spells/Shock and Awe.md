**5th** | Common | Traits: Auditory, Concentrate, Emotion, Fear, Illusion, Manipulate, Mental, Visual
**Range** 100 feet | **Defense** Will

[[Rank 5th]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]] [[Will]]

You create the illusion of cannons exploding, bullets and arrows flying, and magical ballistics firing, as an overwhelming torrent of information, both visual and auditory. Enemies in the area must attempt a Will save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.
**Success** The target is [frightened](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=76) 1.
**Failure** The enemy is frightened 2 and [stunned](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=93) 1.
**Critical Failure** The enemy is frightened 3 and stunned 2.
