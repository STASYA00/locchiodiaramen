[[Spells Index]]

Source: [Figment on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1528)

**1st** | Common | Traits: Cantrip, Concentrate, Illusion, Manipulate, Subtle
**Range** 30 feet

[[Rank 1st]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]]

You create a simple illusory sound or vision. A sound adds the [auditory](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=16) trait to the spell and the sound can't include intelligible words or elaborate music. A vision adds the [visual](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=163) trait, can be no larger than a 5-foot cube, and is clearly crude and undetailed if viewed from within 15 feet. When you Cast or Sustain the Spell, you can attempt to [Create a Diversion](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=45) with the illusion, gaining a +2 circumstance bonus to your [Deception](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=5) check. If the attempt fails against a creature, that creature disbelieves the figment.
