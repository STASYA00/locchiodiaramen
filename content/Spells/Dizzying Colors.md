**1st** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Illusion, Incapacitation, Manipulate, Visual
**Area** 15-foot cone | **Defense** Will

[[Rank 1st]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]] [[Will]]

You unleash a swirling multitude of colors that overwhelms creatures based on their Will saves.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.
**Success** The creature is [dazzled](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=7) for 1 round.
**Failure** The creature is [stunned](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=36) 1, [blinded](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=1) for 1 round, and dazzled for 1 minute.
**Critical Failure** The creature is stunned for 1 round and blinded for 1 minute.
