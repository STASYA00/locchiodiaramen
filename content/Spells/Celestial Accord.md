Source: [Celestial Accord on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1220)

**1st** | Uncommon | Traits: Emotion, Enchantment, Good, Mental, Uncommon
**Range** 30 feet | **Target** 2 creatures that each have an unfriendly or worse attitude toward the other | **Defense** Will

[[Rank 1st]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]] [[Will]]

You intervene in a heated disagreement between two creatures, encouraging them to put aside their differences and find some common ground. The emotional heat of the prior moment becomes only a memory. Each target must make a Will save. A creature currently engaged in combat can't get a result worse than success, and a target that is subject to [hostility](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=300) from any other creature ceases to be affected by _celestial accord_.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.
**Success** The creature is filled with doubt about its own intentions and feels an urge to cooperate with the other. It has a –2 status penalty to attack rolls against the other target for 1 round.
**Failure** The creature can't make hostile actions against the other target and its attitude toward the other target improves to indifferent for the spell's duration.
**Critical Failure** As failure, but the creature's attitude toward the other target improves to [friendly](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=18) for the duration and is [indifferent](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=25) thereafter (until something happens to change that attitude normally).
