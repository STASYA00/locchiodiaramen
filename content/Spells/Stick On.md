Source: [Stick On on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=2829)

**1st** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Manipulate, Morph
**Range** touch | **Target** 1 creature or object

[[Rank 1st]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]]

The skin on your hand begins to ooze a strange adhesive that causes it to stick to a target. You and the target become inseparable for the spell’s duration or until the target [Escapes](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2296). If the creature is smaller than you, it’s [immobilized](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=81) and pulled along with you as you move. If the target is the same size as you, you’re both pulled along with each other as either moves, but you both treat all spaces as [difficult terrain](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2366). If the target is larger than you, you’re immobilized and pulled along as the target moves. You can [Dismiss](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2311) this spell.
