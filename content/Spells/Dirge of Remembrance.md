Source: [Dirge of Remembrance on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1263)

**4th** | Uncommon | Traits: Necromancy, Sonic, Uncommon
**Area** 30-foot cone | **Defense** Will

[[Rank 4th]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]] [[Will]]

You sing a funereal dirge to force restless spirits to fully manifest in the [Material Plane](https://2e.aonprd.com/Planes.aspx?ID=1). Each [incorporeal](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=222) creature in the area takes 4d10 sonic damage (this damage bypasses its resistances) depending on the result of its Will save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.
**Success** The creature takes half damage.
**Failure** The creature takes full damage and becomes semi-corporeal until the spell ends. All attacks against it get the benefit of a [_ghost touch_](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=297) rune.
**Critical Failure** As failure, but double damage.

### Heightened
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d10.
