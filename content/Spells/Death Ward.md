**5th** | Common | Traits: Abjuration
**Target** 1 living creature touched

[[Rank 5th]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]]

You shield a creature from the ravages of negative energy. It receives a +4 status bonus to saves against [death](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=40) and [negative](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=118) effects, gains negative resistance 10, and suppresses the effects of the [doomed](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=9) condition.
