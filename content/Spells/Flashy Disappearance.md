**1st** | Uncommon | Traits: Illusion, Uncommon

[[Rank 1st]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]]

You create a puff of colorful smoke that quickly disperses while you become temporarily [invisible](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=26). You become [undetected](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=39) to all creatures that rely on visual senses unless they can see invisible creatures. You Stride. At the end of your movement, if you have [cover, greater cover](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=459), or [concealment](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=4), attempt a [Stealth](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=15) check to [Hide](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=62). You gain a +2 status bonus to this Stealth check. The invisibility then ends, and you either become [observed](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=27) or [hidden](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=22) to creatures as determined by your check to Hide, if you made one.
