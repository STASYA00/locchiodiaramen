Source: [Mind Games on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=859)

**2nd** | Uncommon | Traits: Enchantment, Mental, Uncommon
**Range** 30 feet | **Target** 1 creature | **Defense** Will

[[Rank 2nd]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]] [[Will]]

You lock minds with the target, trying to mentally subdue them. The target must attempt a Will save. Each time you Sustain this Spell, the target attempts another Will save.

**Critical Success** You are [stunned 1](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=36) and the spell ends.
**Success** The target is unaffected.
**Failure** The target is stunned 1.
**Critical Failure** The target is stunned 2.
