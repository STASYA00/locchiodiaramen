Source: [Darkness on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1480)

**2nd** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Darkness, Manipulate
**Range** 120 feet | **Area** 20-foot burst

[[Rank 2nd]] [[Arcane]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]]

You create a shroud of darkness that prevents light from penetrating or emanating within the area. Light does not enter the area and any non-magical light sources, such as a [torch](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=60) or [lantern](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=29), do not emanate any light while inside the area, even if their light radius would extend beyond the darkness. This also suppresses magical [light](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=100) of your _darkness_ spell's rank or lower. Light can't pass through, so creatures in the area can't see outside. From outside, it appears as a globe of pure darkness.

### Heightened
**Heightened (4th)** Even creatures with [darkvision](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=415) (but not greater darkvision) can barely see through the darkness. They treat targets seen through the darkness as [concealed](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=4).
