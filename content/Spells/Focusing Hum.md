[[Spells Index]]

Source: [Focusing Hum on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=2571)

**3rd** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Manipulate
**Area** 15-foot emanation centered on you

[[Rank 3rd]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]]

You infuse the air around you with a sustained hum that allows creatures within it to resist distractions. The DC of any flat check to Cast a Spell while [stupefied](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=94) for creatures within the area is 2 lower. [Confused](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=63) creatures in the area don't attack anyone, including themselves, and instead lose the ability to speak coherently. You take a –2 penalty to Stealth checks to [Hide](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2404) and [Sneak](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2405).
