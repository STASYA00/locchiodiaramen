[[Spells Index]]

Source: [Perseis's Precautions on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=834)

**3rd** | Uncommon | Traits: Divination, Fortune, Prediction, Uncommon
**Range** touch

[[Rank 3rd]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]]

Leaving nothing to chance, you weave divinatory precautions against an ambush, alerting the target to danger. Once during the duration of this spell, the target can roll one Perception, [Arcana](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=2), [Crafting](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=4), [Lore](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=8), [Occultism](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=11), or [Society](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=14) initiative roll twice and take the better result, after which the spell ends. The strain of staying so prepared is too much for a mind to handle for long; the target is temporarily immune to this spell for 24 hours once the spell ends, whether they used the initiative benefit or not.
