**4th** | Uncommon | Traits: Divination, Uncommon
**Range** 30 feet | **Target** 1 willing creature

[[Rank 4th]] [[Arcane]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]]

You enhance the target's senses. The target gains [low-light vision](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=416), and all of the target's [imprecise](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=412) senses have their distances doubled. If the target already has low-light vision, they gain [darkvision](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=415).

### Heightened
**Heightened (+2)**  The number of targets increases by 1.
