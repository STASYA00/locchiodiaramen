Source: [Runic Weapon on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1658)

**1st** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Manipulate
**Range** touch | **Target** 1 weapon that is unattended or wielded by a willing creature

[[Rank 1st]] [[Arcane]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]]

The weapon glimmers with magic as temporary runes carve down its length. The target becomes a _+1 [striking](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=280)_ weapon, gaining a +1 item bonus to attack rolls and increasing the number of weapon damage dice to two.

### Heightened
**Heightened (6th)** The weapon is _+2 greater striking_.

**Heightened (9th)** The weapon is _+3 major striking_.
