**3rd** | Common | Traits: Polymorph, Transmutation

[[Rank 3rd]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]]

You invoke all things that seep and ooze, your body dissolving into a thick slurry and then reconstituting itself into a Medium ooze battle form. When you first cast this spell, choose black pudding, gelatinous cube, gray ooze, or ochre jelly. While in this form, you gain the [ooze](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=122) trait. You can Dismiss the spell.

 You gain the following statistics and abilities regardless of which battle form you choose: 
AC = 7 + your level. Ignore your armor's check penalty and Speed reduction.20 temporary Hit Points.You are immune to critical hits, precision damage, and [visual](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=163) effects.Resistance 5 to [acid](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=3), piercing, and slashing damage.No vision and motion sense 30 feet. You can sense nearby motion through vibration and air movement as a precise sense.One or more [unarmed](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=199) melee attacks specific to the battle form you choose, which are the only attacks you can Strike with. You're trained with them. Your attack modifier is +14, and you use the listed damage. These attacks are Strength based (for the purpose of the [enfeebled](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=13) condition, for example). If your unarmed attack modifier is higher, you can use it instead.[Athletics](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=3) modifier of +14, unless your own is higher. You also gain specific abilities based on the ooze:

- **[Black Pudding](https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=323)** Speed 15 feet, climb 15 feet; **Melee**  pseudopod, **Damage** 2d6+5 bludgeoning and 1d8 acid.
- **[Gelatinous Cube](https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=321)** Speed 15 feet; **Melee**  cube face, **Damage** 1d6+5 acid; a creature hit by your cube face Strike must succeed at a Fortitude save against your spell DC or be [stunned 1](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=36) (or [paralyzed](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=28) for 1 round on a critical failure); this save has the [incapacitation](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=93) trait.
- **[Gray Ooze](https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=753)** Speed 15 feet, climb 15 feet, swim 15 feet; **Melee**  pseudopod, **Damage** 1d6+5 bludgeoning and 1d6 acid, and you can spend an action after a hit to [Grab](https://2e.aonprd.com/MonsterAbilities.aspx?ID=18) the target.
- **[Ochre Jelly](https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=322)** Speed 15 feet, climb 10 feet; resistance 5 to [electricity](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=56); **Melee**  pseudopod, **Damage** 1d8+5 bludgeoning and 1d8 acid, and you can spend an action after a hit to [Grab](https://2e.aonprd.com/MonsterAbilities.aspx?ID=18) the target.

### Heightened
**Heightened (4th)** Your battle form is Large, and your attacks have 10-foot [reach](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=192). You must have enough space to expand into or the spell is lost. You instead gain 30 temporary HP; resistance 7 to acid, piercing, and slashing; attack modifier +16; damage bonus +9; and Athletics +16. Your motion sense has a range of 40 feet.

**Heightened (5th)** Your battle form is Huge, and your attacks have 15-foot reach. You must have enough space to expand into or the spell is lost. You instead gain 40 temporary HP; resistance 10 to acid, piercing, and slashing; attack modifier +18; damage bonus +6 and double damage dice; and Athletics +20. Your motion sense has a range of 60 feet.

**Heightened (8th)** Your battle form is Gargantuan (20-foot-by-20-foot space), and your attacks have 20-foot reach. You must have enough space to expand into or the spell is lost. You instead gain 60 temporary HP; resistance 12 to acid, piercing, and slashing; attack modifier +28; damage bonus +13 and double damage dice; and Athletics +29. Your motion sense has a range of 60 feet.
