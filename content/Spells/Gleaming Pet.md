Source: [Gleaming Pet on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=2721)

**3rd** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Light, Manipulate, Visual
**Range** 30 feet | **Target** your companion or eidolon | **Defense** Fortitude

[[Rank 3rd]] [[Arcane]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]] [[Fortitude]]

The target glows intensely with an otherworldly light, blinding any who approach. Any creature that begins its turn adjacent to the target must attempt a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected and is immune for 10 minutes.
**Success** The creature is unaffected.
**Failure** The creature is [dazzled](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=65) for 1 round.
**Critical Failure** The creature is [blinded](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=59) for 1 round.
