Source: [Dispel Magic on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1493)

**2nd** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Manipulate
**Range** 120 feet | **Target** 1 spell effect or unattended magic item

[[Rank 2nd]] [[Arcane]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]]

You unravel the magic behind a spell or effect. Attempt a [counteract](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=371) check against the target. If you successfully counteract a magic item, it becomes a mundane item of its type for 10 minutes. This doesn't change the item's non-magical properties. If the target is an [artifact](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=253) or similar item, you automatically fail.
