**Range** 30 feet | **Area** 10-foot {{rules 2387 "emanation"}} | **Defense** basic Reflex

[[Rank 3rd]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]] [[Reflex]]

You teleport to an unoccupied space within range, leaving behind a faint echo of your form that explodes with magical force. Each creature within a 10-foot emanation from your previous location takes 4d8 [force](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=610) damage.

### Heightened
**Heightened (+2)** The range increases by 10 feet, and the damage increases by 3d8.
