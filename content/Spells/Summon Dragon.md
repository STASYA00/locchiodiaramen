Source: [Summon Dragon on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1697)

**5th** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Manipulate, Summon
**Range** 30 feet

[[Rank 5th]] [[Arcane]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]]

You summon a creature that has the [dragon](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=50) trait and whose level is 5 or lower to fight for you. If the dragon has a magical tradition trait ([arcane](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=11), [divine](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=48), [occult](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=120), or [primal](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=134)), you can summon it only if you're using that tradition to cast summon dragon.
