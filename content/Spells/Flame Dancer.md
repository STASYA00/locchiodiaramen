[[Spells Index]]

Source: [Flame Dancer on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1356)

**5th** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Fire, Manipulate
**Range** 60 feet | **Target** 1 willing creature

[[Rank 5th]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]]

Fire encircles the target's hands and feet, and its eyes and hair catch ablaze. The target's unarmed Strikes deal an additional 2d6 fire damage. Critical hits with these Strikes also deal 2d4 [persistent fire damage](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=29). Additionally, when the target attempts to [Demoralize](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=53) a foe, they can use [Performance](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=12) instead of [Intimidation](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=7); when using Performance to Demoralize, the action loses the [auditory](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=16) trait but gains the [visual](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=163) trait, and the target doesn't take a penalty when attempting to Demoralize a creature that doesn't understand its language.

### Heightened
**Heightened (7th)** The fire damage increases to 3d6, and the persistent fire damage increases to 3d4. If the target gets a critical success on a Demoralize check using Performance, the target of the Demoralize check becomes [fleeing](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=17) from the target of _flame dancer_ for 1 round.
