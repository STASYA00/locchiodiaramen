Source: [Haunting Spirit on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=2727)

**4th** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Manipulate, Spirit
**Range** 60 feet | **Target** 1 living creature | **Defense** Will

[[Rank 4th]] [[Occult]] [[Will]]

You call an [invisible](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=83) spirit that only the target can see to haunt them. At the start of each of the target’s turns, it must attempt a Will saving throw. The target can spend its entire turn running as far away as possible to escape the spirit and end the spell early.

**Critical Success** The spell immediately ends.
**Success** The target becomes [off-guard](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=58) for 1 round.
**Failure** As success, and the target takes 4d8 spirit damage.
**Critical Failure** As success, but the target takes 8d8 spirit damage.

### Heightened
**Heightened (+1)** The damage is increased by 1d8 (2d8 on a critical failure).
