**5th** | Rare | Traits: Concentrate, Light, Manipulate, Mythic, Rare, Sanctified

[[Rank 5th]] [[Arcane]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]]

You don a diadem of radiant light, which sheds [bright light](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2402) to a range of 60 feet and [dim light](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2403) to a further 60 feet. When you Cast the Spell and when you [Sustain](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2317) it during the duration, you can draw a disc of spiraling light from the diadem and throw it at a creature within 120 feet. Make a ranged spell attack at mythic proficiency against the target's AC. This action has the [attack](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=540) and [spirit](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=737) traits. On a hit, you deal 4d8 spirit damage, 1d4 [persistent spirit damage](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=86), and the target is [dazzled](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=65) for 1 round (3 rounds on a critical hit). The persistent damage isn't doubled on a critical hit. 

If the disc passes through an area of magical [darkness](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=569) or targets a creature affected by magical darkness, the disc's glow attempts to [counteract](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2250) the darkness using your [Religion](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=46) or [Occultism](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=44) skill modifier as the counteract check modifier and half your level as the counteract rank.

### Heightened
**Heightened (+2)** The disc’s divine damage increases by 2d8, and the persistent damage increases by 1d4.
