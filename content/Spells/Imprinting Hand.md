**2nd** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Manipulate
**Range** touch | **Target** 1 living or undead creature | **Defense** Fortitude

[[Rank 2nd]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]] [[Fortitude]]

Your hand presses into your target, leaving an imprint on its body. The target takes 8 force damage with a Fortitude save. The first time within the next minute the target's Hit Points are restored by a [healing](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=623) effect, that healing is reduced by 8. After any amount of healing is reduced, the spell ends.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.
**Success** The target takes half the force damage and half the healing reduction.
**Failure** The target takes the full force damage and the full healing reduction.
**Critical Failure** The target takes double the damage and double the healing reduction.

### Heightened
**Heightened (+1)** The damage and healing reduction both increase by 4.
