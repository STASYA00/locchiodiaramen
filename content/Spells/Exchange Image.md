**1st** | Uncommon | Traits: Illusion, Uncommon, Visual
**Range** touch | **Target** you and 1 other humanoid | **Defense** Will

[[Rank 1st]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]] [[Will]]

To mislead pursuers, the Lacunafex developed the means to swap visages. You trade appearances with the target, with the effects depending on the result of the target’s Will saving throw. Willing and unconscious targets automatically critically fail this saving throw.

**Critical Success** No effect.
**Success** You take on the target’s appearance, and they take yours. This has the same effects as a 1st-level [_illusory disguise_](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=159) spell, except that the target can’t [Dismiss](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=74) the disguise. The duration is 1 minute or until Dismissed.
**Failure** As success, but the duration is 1 hour or until Dismissed.
**Critical Failure** As success, but the duration is 24 hours.
