**1st** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Manipulate
**Range** touch | **Target** 1 willing creature

[[Rank 1st]] [[Arcane]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]]

You protect your target from going astray in hostile territory by tracking where they've already been, helping the target deduce where they still need to go. The target leaves a glittering trail behind them that lasts for the spell's duration. This trail doesn't denote the direction or the order of its path—it merely indicates where the target has moved during the spell's duration.

### Heightened
**Heightened (2nd)** The duration increases to 8 hours.

**Heightened (3rd)** The duration increases to last until your next daily preparations.
