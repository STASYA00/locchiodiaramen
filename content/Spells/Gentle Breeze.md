Source: [Gentle Breeze on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1316)

**2nd** | Common | Traits: Air, Concentrate, Healing, Manipulate, Vitality
**Range** 60 feet | **Area** 40-foot burst

[[Rank 2nd]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]]

A light, soothing breeze flows through the area, making it easier to rest and recover. [Medicine](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=9) checks attempted to benefit living creatures in the area get a +2 status bonus. Any living creature in the area also gets a +2 status bonus to saving throws against afflictions and, if it remains within the area for the full duration, regains 10 Hit Points. In addition, the cool breeze reduces the [temperature effects of heat](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=642) by one step for any creature in the area.

### Heightened
**Heightened (+2)** The healing increases by 10 Hit Points.
