Source: [Caster's Imposition on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1215)

**3rd** | Uncommon | Traits: Abjuration, Curse, Uncommon
**Target** 1 creature within 60 feet | **Defense** Will

[[Rank 3rd]] [[Arcane]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]] [[Will]]

A magical interference prevents the target from contributing to any form of cooperative spellcasting. On a failed save, the target can't participate in any ritual unless they can cast the ritual alone, and they can't access any spells provided by a [coven](https://2e.aonprd.com/MonsterAbilities.aspx?ID=11) or similar cooperative ability.

### Heightened
**Heightened (4th)** Increase the duration to 1 hour.

**Heightened (5th)** Increase the duration to 1 day.
