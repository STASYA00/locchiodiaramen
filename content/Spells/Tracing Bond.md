Source: [Tracing Bond on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=2849)

**2nd** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Detection, Manipulate
**Range** touch | **Target** you and 1 willing creature

[[Rank 2nd]] [[Arcane]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]]

You and an ally might be parting ways, but you know that you’ll continue to have each other’s backs. For the duration of the spell, you and the ally always know the direction and distance to the other’s location as long as you’re both on the same plane of existence. While this bond doesn’t allow you to send messages or generally know the other person’s status, at any point during the spell, either of you can send a single distress signal to the other as a single action that has the [concentrate](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=561) and [mental](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=647) traits. This signal is a simple mental alert that doesn’t provide any additional details about the situation or status of the person. Once this signal has been used, neither target can send another signal for the duration of the spell.
