**4th** | Rare | Traits: Concentrate, Light, Manipulate, Rare
**Defense** Will

[[Rank 4th]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]] [[Will]]

The golden light of a new dawn and the promise of life beyond death radiates from your body. You radiate [bright light](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2402) in a 20-foot radius (and [dim light](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2403) for the next 20 feet) and gain mental resistance 5. Any [nindoru](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=739) [fiend](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=600) or [undead](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=722) that begins its turn adjacent to you must attempt a Will save with the following results.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected and immune to this spell’s effects for the remainder of the spell’s duration.
**Success** The creature is [sickened](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=91) 1 until the end of its turn.
**Failure** The creature is sickened 1. In addition, it becomes [slowed](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=92) 1 until the end of its next turn.
**Critical Failure** As failure, but sickened 2 and the creature remains slowed 1 for the rest of the spell’s duration.

### Heightened
**Heightened (6th)** You gain mental resistance 10.

**Heightened (8th)** You gain mental resistance 15.
