Source: [Reed Whistle on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=2447)

**1st** | Uncommon | Traits: Concentrate, Manipulate, Plant, Uncommon, Wood

[[Rank 1st]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]]

You enchant a blade of grass that you can easily hold in your mouth without inhibiting your speech or other actions. As a reaction, you can reduce the spell’s remaining duration by 1 hour to [Point Out](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2315) a creature you detect as you sharply whistle through the reed. You and your allies also gain a +2 circumstance bonus to [Perception](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2298) checks to [Seek](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2301) the creature for 1d4 rounds.

### Heightened
**Heightened (3rd)** The spell’s duration becomes 4 hours.
