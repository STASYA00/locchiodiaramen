Source: [Scouring Pulse on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1124)

**5th** | Uncommon | Traits: Evocation, Light, Positive, Uncommon, Vitality
**Range** 120 feet | **Defense** Reflex

[[Rank 5th]] [[Arcane]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]] [[Reflex]]

You inundate the area with concentrated positive energy, which scours away the stain of undeath. Each creature in the area takes 6d8 positive damage and must attempt a [basic](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=329) Reflex save. Creatures that fail the save also take 1d8 [persistent positive damage](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=29). Creatures that take persistent positive damage from _scouring pulse_ are wreathed in a heatless halo of light. They emit bright light in a 5-foot radius preventing them from being [concealed](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=4); if the creature is [invisible](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=26), they're concealed while affected by scouring pulse, rather than being [undetected](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=39).

### Heightened
**Heightened (8th)**  The positive damage increases to 9d8 damage, and the persistent positive damage increases to 2d8. Creatures remain suffused in light for 1d4 additional rounds after removing the persistent positive damage.
