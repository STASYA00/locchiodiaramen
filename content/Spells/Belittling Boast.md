**5th** | Uncommon | Traits: Auditory, Emotion, Enchantment, Fear, Mental, Uncommon

[[Rank 5th]] [[Arcane]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]]

You boast about your skills or combat prowess, bolstering your own abilities and belittling your opponents. Select one of the following to boast about: melee attack rolls, ranged attack rolls, spell attack rolls, or a single skill. Attempt an [Intimidation](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=7) check to [Demoralize](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=53) all enemies within 30 feet. Each creature that becomes [frightened](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=19) additionally takes a –1 circumstance penalty to the attack roll or skill you boasted about for 1 minute. If at least one creature becomes frightened, you gain a +1 status bonus to the attack roll or skill you boasted about for 1 minute.

### Heightened
**Heightened (8th)** The bonus and penalty increase to 2.
