Source: [Fated Healing on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=2539)

**1st** | Uncommon | Traits: Concentrate, Linguistic, Manipulate, Mental, Uncommon
**Range** 30 feet | **Target** creatures who are hostile toward each other

[[Rank 1st]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]]

You speak about the consequences of actions people take against each other and how it’s possible to break cycles of violence simply by making a different choice. The targets regain 1d4 Hit Points at the end of each of their own turns while the spell is in effect. If a target uses a [hostile action](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2251) against the other target, the spell ends for the target that used the hostile action.

### Heightened
**Heightened (+1)** The targets regain an additional 1d4 Hit Points at the end of their own turns.
