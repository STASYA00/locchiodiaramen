Source: [Eat Fire on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1352)

**1st** | Common | Traits: Cantrip, Fire, Manipulate

[[Rank 1st]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]]

You consume and ingest fire, making it less harmful to you. You gain resistance 5 to fire damage until the end of the current turn. During the remaining duration of the spell, you can use the Belch Smoke action. The spell ends if you fall [unconscious](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=38), inhale, or exhale (this includes speaking).

 **Belch Smoke**  You exhale what remains of the smoke, ending the spell and creating a smoke cloud in a 5-foot burst within 20 feet. All creatures within the smoke cloud are [concealed](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=4), and all other creatures are concealed to them. The smoke lasts for 1 minute or until dispersed by a strong wind.

### Heightened
**Heightened (+3)** The resistance increases by 5.
