Source: [Necromancer's Generosity on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=2775)

**1st** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Manipulate, Void
**Range** 30 feet | **Target** 1 undead minion you control

[[Rank 1st]] [[Arcane]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]]

You channel void energy through your magical connection to your undead minion to strengthen the creature. The target regains 1d8+4 Hit Points when you Cast the Spell, and it gains a +2 status bonus to saves against [vitality](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=509) effects for the duration.

### Heightened
**Heightened (+1)** The amount of healing increases by 1d8+4.
