Source: [Deafness on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1483)

**2nd** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Manipulate
**Range** 30 feet | **Target** 1 creature | **Defense** Fortitude

[[Rank 2nd]] [[Arcane]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]] [[Fortitude]]

The target loses its hearing. It must attempt a Fortitude save, and it is then temporarily immune for 1 minute.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.
**Success** The target is [deafened](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=8) for 1 round.
**Failure** The target is deafened for 10 minutes.
**Critical Failure** The target is deafened permanently.
