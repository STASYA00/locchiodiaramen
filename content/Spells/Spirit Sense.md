Source: [Spirit Sense on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=2028)

**2nd** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Detection, Manipulate, Mental
**Area** 30-foot emanation

[[Rank 2nd]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]]

You open your mind to the metaphysical, enabling you to sense nearby spirits. Even if you aren't [Searching](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2630), you get a check to find [haunts](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=622) and [spirits](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=737) in the area. You gain a +1 status bonus to the following checks regarding haunts or spirits: Perception checks to [Seek](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2301), attempts to [Recall Knowledge](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2367), skill checks to determine the reason for their existence, and skill checks to disable a haunt. You also gain a +1 status bonus to AC and saving throws against haunts and spirits.

### Heightened
**Heightened (6th)** The spell's duration lasts until the next time you make your daily preparations.
