Source: [Hypnotize on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1564)

**3rd** | Common | Traits: Illusion, Manipulate, Subtle, Visual
**Range** 120 feet | **Area** 10-foot burst | **Defense** Will

[[Rank 3rd]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]] [[Will]]

You create a cloud of mesmerizing patterns and colors that hovers in the air. Creatures are [dazzled](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=7) while inside the cloud. In addition, a creature must attempt a Will saving throw if it is inside the cloud when you cast it, when it enters the cloud, when it ends its turn within the cloud, or if it uses a [Seek](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=84) or Interact action on the cloud. A creature currently [fascinated](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=14) by _hypnotize_ doesn't attempt new saves.

**Success** The target is unaffected.
**Failure** The target is fascinated by the cloud.
**Critical Failure** The target is fascinated by the cloud. While it remains fascinated, it can't use reactions.
