**2nd** | Common | Traits: Auditory, Concentrate, Emotion, Fear, Manipulate, Mental
**Range** 30 feet | **Target** 1 creature | **Defense** Will

[[Rank 2nd]] [[Occult]] [[Will]]

A heap of insults and invectives spew from your mouth—words so devastating your foes burn from the intensity of your diatribe. Your words deal 2d6 [persistent fire damage](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=29), and the target must attempt a Will save. If the target doesn't understand the language or you're not speaking a language, it gains a +4 circumstance bonus to its save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.
**Success** The target takes half the persistent fire damage.
**Failure** The target becomes [frightened 1](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=76) and takes the full persistent fire damage.
**Critical Failure** The target becomes frightened 2 and takes double the persistent fire damage.

### Heightened
**Heightened (+2)** You can target two additional creatures, and the persistent damage increases by 2d6.
