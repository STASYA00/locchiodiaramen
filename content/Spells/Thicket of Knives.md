**1st** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Illusion, Manipulate, Visual

[[Rank 1st]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]]

You create numerous phantom copies of your weapon arm, hiding your true movements and rendering your attacks unpredictable. You gain a +2 status bonus to [Deception](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=38) checks. If you’re untrained in Deception, you can use the [Feint](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2390) action anyway as well as add your level as your proficiency bonus despite being untrained.
