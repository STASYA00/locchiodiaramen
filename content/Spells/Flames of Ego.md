**5th** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Fire, Incapacitation, Light, Manipulate, Mental
**Range** 120 feet | **Target** 1 creature | **Defense** Will

[[Rank 5th]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]] [[Will]]

Elegant flames of incredible beauty coruscate across the target's body, creating overconfidence and carelessness. The target attempts a Will save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.
**Success** The target sheds [bright light](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=407) in a 20-foot emanation (and [dim light](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=408) to the next 40 feet) and becomes [fascinated](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=14) with itself for 1 round. It must spend at least 1 action on its turn on a taunting display of arrogance or overconfidence. Using actions that include such a display—such as using [Perform](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=59) to show off—count toward this requirement.
**Failure** As success, but the duration is 1 minute and the target can't act hostile toward a creature until that creature [acts hostile](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=300) toward the target.
**Critical Failure** As failure, except the target must spend at least 2 actions on its turn on a taunting display of arrogance or overconfidence.
