Source: [Etheric Shards on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=2569)

**5th** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Force, Manipulate
**Range** 60 feet | **Defense** basic Reflex

[[Rank 5th]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]] [[Reflex]]

You reach out and sow glittering splinters of force throughout the area, otherworldly caltrops of will and ectoplasm. The area is [difficult terrain](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2366), even to flying or incorporeal creatures. Any creature that moves through the area takes 1d4 piercing damage per square traversed, with a basic Reflex save made at the end of each move action they take through the area.

### Heightened
**Heightened (+2)** The spell’s area increases by 5 feet, and the piercing damage increases by 1.
