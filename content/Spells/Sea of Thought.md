Source: [Sea of Thought on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=2579)

**3rd** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Manipulate
**Range** 60 feet | **Area** 15-foot burst | **Defense** Fortitude

[[Rank 3rd]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]] [[Fortitude]]

You cover the area in a sloshing torrent of semi-solidified thought, roughly ankle high. The area becomes [difficult terrain](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2366), similarly to a shallow bog. Each round that a creature starts its turn in the area, it must attempt a Fortitude save against the shifting waves of thought. On a failure, it takes a –10-foot circumstance penalty to its Speeds until it leaves the area, and on a critical failure, it is also knocked [prone](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=88).
