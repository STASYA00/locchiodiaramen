[[Spells Index]]

Source: [Drop Dead on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1989)

**5th** | Uncommon | Traits: Illusion, Manipulate, Subtle, Uncommon, Visual
**Range** 120 feet | **Target** 1 creature

[[Rank 5th]] [[Arcane]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]]

The target appears to fall down dead, though it actually turns [invisible](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=83). Its illusory corpse remains where it fell, complete with a believable fatal wound. This illusion looks and feels like a dead body. If the target's death seems absurd—for instance, a barbarian at full health appears to be slain by 2 damage—the GM can grant the attacker an immediate Perception check to disbelieve the illusion. If the target uses [hostile actions](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=300), the spell ends. This ends the entire spell, so the illusory corpse disappears too.

### Heightened
**Heightened (7th)** The spell doesn't end if the target uses a hostile action.
