Source: [Winning Streak on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=2869)

**4th** | Common | Traits: Concentrate
**Range** 20 feet | **Target** 1 creature

[[Rank 4th]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]]

The target is energized by their good fortune as it spreads to others—as long as they keep winning. The target gains the [quickened](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=89) condition for 1 round. If they or one of their allies within 20 feet gets a critical success on an attack roll against a significant foe, whoever got the critical success becomes quickened if they weren’t already, and the duration of the winning streak is extended by another round. Creatures quickened by the spell can use the extra action to Step, Stride, or Strike. If a full round passes without the quickened creature or one of their allies within 20 feet critically succeeding on an attack roll against a significant foe, the _winning streak_ breaks, and the spell ends.
