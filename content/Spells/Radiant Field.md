**2nd** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Light, Manipulate
**Range** 120 feet | **Area** 20-foot {{rules 2385 "burst"}} | **Defense** Fortitude

[[Rank 2nd]] [[Arcane]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]] [[Fortitude]]

You create an area of [bright light](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2402). Creatures with [light blindness](https://2e.aonprd.com/MonsterAbilities.aspx?ID=68) that are [blinded](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=59) by radiant field and remain in the area must attempt a Fortitude save at the start of their turns. On a failure, a creature remains blinded for 1 round; this is an [incapacitation](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=631) effect. This spell also suppresses magical [darkness](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2404) of your radiant field spell’s rank or lower.

### Heightened
**Heightened (4th)** Creatures seen through the area are [concealed](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=62) to creatures outside the area. Creatures with light blindness can continue to be blinded by the field as long as the field is visible, even when outside of the field.
