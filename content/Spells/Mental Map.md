**2nd** | Uncommon | Traits: Concentrate, Mental, Uncommon
**Range** touch | **Target** 1 creature | **Defense** Will

[[Rank 2nd]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]] [[Will]]

You glean detailed information about a specific place visited by a creature directly from its mind, unless it fends you off with a Will save. The target can choose to fail the save.

**Success** The target is unaffected.
**Failure** You gain critical information about the place in question, granting you a +2 circumstance bonus to Survival checks while in the location and any skill checks to [Recall Knowledge](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2367) about the location for the next 24 hours.
**Critical Failure** As failure, but you can erase all knowledge of the location from the target for 24 hours if desired.
