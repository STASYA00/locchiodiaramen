**4th** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Curse, Manipulate
**Range** 30 feet | **Target** 1 creature | **Defense** Fortitude

[[Rank 4th]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]] [[Fortitude]]

You inflict a curse upon the target that causes any wound to gush blood. The target must attempt a Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.
**Success** For 1 minute, the first time each round that the target would take at least 10 piercing or slashing damage from a single Strike, the strike deals an additional 2d6 damage of the same type.
**Failure** As success, but the Strike also deals an additional 2d6 [persistent bleed damage](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=29).

### Heightened
**Heightened (+2)** The additional damage increases by 1d6, and the persistent bleed damage increases by 1d6.
