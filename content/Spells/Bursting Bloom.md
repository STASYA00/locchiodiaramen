**4th** | Uncommon | Traits: Conjuration, Plant, Uncommon
**Range** 60 feet | **Target** 1 creature | **Defense** Fortitude

[[Rank 4th]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]] [[Fortitude]]

You cause a rose bush to sprout from the chest of a foe, bursting out of its body and wrapping it in thorned vines. The target takes 6d6 piercing damage and 1d6 [persistent bleed damage](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=29) and must attempt a Fortitude save. When the target is no longer taking persistent bleed damage and is no longer [encumbered](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=12), the rose bush withers away to nothing.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.
**Success** The creature takes half damage and no persistent damage.
**Failure** The creature takes full damage and persistent damage and is encumbered for 4 rounds.
**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage and persistent damage and is encumbered for 1 minute.

### Heightened
**Heightened (+2)** The initial damage increases by 3d6, and the persistent bleed damage increases by 1d6.
