**2nd** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Illusion, Manipulate, Visual
**Range** touch | **Target** 1 creature

[[Rank 2nd]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]]

The target's form appears blurry. It becomes [concealed](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=4). As the nature of this effect still leaves the target's location obvious, the target can't use this concealment to [Hide](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=62) or [Sneak](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=63).
