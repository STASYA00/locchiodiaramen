**1st** | Common | Traits: Auditory, Concentrate, Manipulate
**Range** 60 feet | **Target** 1 creature

[[Rank 1st]] [[Occult]]

You call out a liberating cry, urging an ally to break free of an effect that holds them in place. If the target is [grabbed](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=77), [immobilized](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=81), or [restrained](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=90), it can immediately use a reaction to attempt to [Escape](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2296).
