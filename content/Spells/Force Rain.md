**Range** 60 feet | **Area** varies | **Defense** basic Reflex

[[Rank 4th]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]] [[Reflex]]

You conjure a magical cloud that batters creatures with shards of solidified magic. Creatures in the spell's area take force damage with a [basic](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=329) Reflex save. The number of actions you spend when Casting this Spell determines the area and other parameters.

  This spell affects a single 5-foot square and deals 4d6 force damage.  This spell affects all squares in a 10-foot burst and deals 8d6 force damage.  The shards home in on creatures. This spell affects all squares in a 10-foot burst. Creatures in the area don't attempt a saving throw and instead automatically take 20 force damage.

### Heightened
**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d6 for the 1-action version, by 2d6 for the 2-action version, and by 5 for the 3-action version.
