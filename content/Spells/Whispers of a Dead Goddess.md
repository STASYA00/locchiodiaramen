Source: [Whispers of a Dead Goddess on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=2625)

**5th** | Common | Traits: Auditory, Concentrate, Incapacitation, Manipulate, Mental
**Range** 60 feet | **Defense** Will

[[Rank 5th]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]] [[Will]]

The mysterious whispers that fill the Mordant Spire, which some believe to be the voice of the dead goddess [Acavna](https://2e.aonprd.com/Deities.aspx?ID=402), become audible. A creature in the area when this [Spell is Cast](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2734) or who enters the area must attempt a Will save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected and becomes temporarily immune for 1 hour.
**Success** The target becomes [stunned](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=93) 1, listening to the whispers.
**Failure** As success, and the creature will not voluntarily leave the area for 1 round.
**Critical Failure** As failure, and you can whisper a single course of action to each creature that critically failed, with the effects of [_suggestion_](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1693). The effect ends on a creature if you stop sustaining the spell or the creature leaves the area.
