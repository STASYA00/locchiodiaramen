[[Spells Index]]

Source: [Familiar’s Face on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1991)

**3rd** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Manipulate, Scrying
**Range** 1 mile | **Target** 1 willing creature that’s your animal companion or familiar

[[Rank 3rd]] [[Arcane]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]]

The target becomes a scrying sensor, allowing you to see through its eyes, smell what it smells, and similarly use its other senses. If you Cast a Spell with the [revelation](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=686) trait that affects your senses, such as [_see the unseen_](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1663), while this spell is active, you gain the benefit of the spell through the target's senses instead of your own. You can also speak through the target with a voice much like yours, though it takes on some of the timbre and character of the target's growls or squawks. You can use [Command an Animal](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2400) on the target as part of Sustaining this spell. You don't need line of sight or line of effect to your target when you Cast this Spell.
