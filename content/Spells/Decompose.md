**1st** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Manipulate
**Range** touch | **Target** 1 Large or smaller corpse

[[Rank 1st]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]]

You touch a corpse to speed up the decaying process. The target begins to rapidly decompose. After a day, any organic material will be gone, and after a week, any trace of the target will be gone.

### Heightened
**Heightened (3rd)** Organic material is gone within a minute, and any trace of the target is gone within an hour.
