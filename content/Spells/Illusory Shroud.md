Source: [Illusory Shroud on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1287)

**2nd** | Uncommon | Traits: Illusion, Uncommon, Visual
**Range** touch | **Target** 1 willing creature

[[Rank 2nd]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]]

You shroud the target in subtle illusions that make it difficult to detect. The target gains a +1 status bonus to [Stealth](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=15) checks to [Hide](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=62) or [Sneak](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=63). For the duration, the target can become [hidden](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=22) to a creature even if they don't have [cover](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=459) and aren't otherwise [concealed](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=4) from that creature. Even if the target isn't concealed or doesn't have cover or greater cover at the end of a Sneak action, the target isn't automatically [observed](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=27).
