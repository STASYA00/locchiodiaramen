[[Rank 2nd]] [[Arcane]] [[Divine]] [[Occult]] [[Reflex]]

You fire a torrent of jagged bone shards from your hands that rips through the fleshy bodies of your enemies. The spray deals 2d10 piercing damage and 1 [persistent bleed damage](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=29) to all creatures in the area, with a [basic](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=329) Reflex save.

### Heightened
**Heightened (+1)** The piercing damage increases by 1d10, and the persistent bleed damage increases by 1.
