[[Spells Index]]

Source: [Cloak of Colors on AoN](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1977)

**5th** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Manipulate, Visual
**Range** 30 feet | **Target** 1 creature

[[Rank 5th]] [[Arcane]] [[Occult]]

A cloak of swirling colors shrouds the target. Creatures are [dazzled](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=65) while adjacent to it, and attacking the target causes a brilliant flash of light. A creature that hits the target with a melee attack must attempt a Will save. The creature is then temporarily immune until the end of its turn; this effect has the [incapacitation](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=631) trait.

**Success** The attacker is unaffected.
**Failure** The attacker is [blinded](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=59) for 1 round.
**Critical Failure** The attacker is [stunned](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=93) for 1 round.
