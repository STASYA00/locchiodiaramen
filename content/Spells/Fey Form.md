**4th** | Common | Traits: Concentrate, Manipulate, Polymorph

[[Rank 4th]] [[Occult]] [[Primal]]

Channeling energy from the mischievous [First World](https://2e.aonprd.com/Planes.aspx?ID=35), you transform into a Medium fey battle form. When you Cast this Spell, choose dryad, elananx, naiad, redcap, or unicorn. If you choose redcap, the battle form is Small. If you choose unicorn, the battle form is Large, and you must have space to expand into, or the spell is lost. While in this form, you gain the [fey](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=599) trait. Unless you choose elananx or unicorn, you have hands in this battle form and can use [manipulate](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=645) actions. You can [Dismiss](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2311) the spell.

 You gain the following statistics and abilities regardless of the form that you choose: 
AC = 19 + your level. Ignore your armor's check penalty and Speed reduction.15 [temporary Hit Points](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2321) and [weakness](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2317) 5 to [cold iron](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=2916).[Low-light vision](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=416).One or more unarmed melee attacks specific to the battle form you choose, which are the only attacks you can [Strike](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=1167) with. You're trained with them. Your attack modifier is +16, and your damage bonus is +8. These attacks are Dexterity based. If your attack modifier is higher for the given unarmed attack or weapon, you can use it instead.[Acrobatics](https://2e.aonprd.com/Skills.aspx?ID=34) modifiers of +16, unless your own modifier is higher.You also gain specific abilities based on the type of fey:

- **[Dryad](https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=3112)** Speed 25 feet; weakness 5 to fire; the temporary HP gained increases to 30; **Melee**  branch, **Damage** 1d12 bludgeoning.
- **[Elananx](https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=2972)** Speed 30 feet; resistance 5 to fire; **Melee**  jaws, **Damage** 1d6 piercing plus 1d6 fire; **Melee**  claw ([agile](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=526)), **Damage** 1d6 slashing.
- **[Naiad](https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=3111)** Speed 25 feet, swim 25 feet; resistance 5 to fire; **Melee**  aqueous fist ([water](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=732)), **Damage** 1d8 bludgeoning.
- **[Redcap](https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=3165)** Speed 40 feet; **Melee**  scythe ([deadly d10](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=4472), [trip](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2382)), **Damage** 1d10 slashing; **Melee**  boot ([agile](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=526), [versatile](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=724) B), **Damage** 1d6 piercing.
- **[Unicorn](https://2e.aonprd.com/Monsters.aspx?ID=3223)** Speed 40 feet; resistance 5 to [poison](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=669); gain the [beast](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=547) and [holy](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=517) traits in addition to fey; **Melee**  horn ([holy](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=517)), **Damage** 1d10 piercing plus 1 spirit; **Melee**  hoof ([agile](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=526)), **Damage** 1d8 bludgeoning; all Strikes have the effects of a _[ghost touch](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=2840)_ property rune.
